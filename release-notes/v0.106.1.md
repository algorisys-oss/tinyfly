# v0.106.1

**Release Date:** 2026-10-12

Keep upper legs inside clothes during turnarounds and moving poses across the
family, work, anime and manga cast.

## Fixed

- Bare thighs and trouser roots no longer overwrite shirt, hoodie or cardigan
  hems when a leg swings toward the viewer.
- Shirt waists make room for wider hips and thigh thickness.
- Skirts, sari drapes, aprons and coat skirts use the body's resolved drawing
  order, including ties, leaning poses and camera views.
- Festive dandiya tunics and decorated skirts follow the same resolved order
  in the live example and its headless scene.
- Camera-view part ordering is relative to the character's root depth, so
  translating the camera does not change which body parts overlap.

## Validation

- Pixel coverage checks exercise every cast preset through full turns with
  moving legs and all built-in upper garments.
- Camera translation and split-part rendering checks cover trousers, shorts,
  skirts and all over-garments. Exposed shins and feet remain visible.
- Visual review compares the wardrobe through eight turnaround views.

## Upgrade Notes

- Existing outfit data works unchanged. No preset-specific corrections are
  required in animations or the editor.
- Custom `layers.parts[id].clip` hooks clip the bare body before `over` layers;
  `joints.parts[id].order` exposes the resolved drawing index.
