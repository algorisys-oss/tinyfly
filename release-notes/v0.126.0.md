# v0.126.0 — Doors open from the handle side

Released: 2026-11-16

In the Home Time example the figure walked up from the left and opened the
house's door from its hinge: the door hung on its left edge, swung out over
the figure, and the doorway opened on the far side. A house's door can now
hang on either edge and swing either way, and has a handle to grab.

## Added

- **`house({ door: { hinge, opens } })`**: the front door hangs on its
  `'left'` or `'right'` edge (as seen from the front) and opens `'out'` or
  `'in'`. The defaults, left and out, are the door as it was.
- **`handle` anchor** on a house: on the door's free edge, at half its
  height. Aim a figure's `grab` at `anchor:handle`.

## Fixed

- **Home Time**: the door hangs on the right and swings into the house, and
  the figure takes it by the handle, on its own side.

## Docs

- `docs/props.md`, `docs/llm-guide.md` and the tinyfly skill: hang a door on
  the side away from the figure that opens it, and grab its handle.

## Validation

- Unit test: the handle sits on the free edge for either hinge, and the open
  door's free edge comes out of the wall or goes into the house as asked.
- Home Time rendered frame by frame before and after.
