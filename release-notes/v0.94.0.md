# v0.94.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-07

Stick figures get behaviours of your own. Write new actions from existing
beats or as timed pose steps, add gaits, and bundle a recurring character's
look, acting style, gait, usual face and stance into a persona.

## Added

- **`defineAction()`**: a new beat action, written either as beats built from
  other actions (`beats: (beat) => [...]`) or as timed pose steps from the
  current pose, like a gag (`steps: (from, beat) => [{ after, pose, easing? }]`).
  `needs` declares the fields it requires, and they're checked like the
  built-in actions'.
- **`defineGait()`**: a new gait as data (swing, knee, arm, elbow, lean and the
  rest of the `Gait` fields, plus `cycle`: ms per two steps).
- **Casts**: `scriptTracks(id, beats, { actions, gaits })`,
  `checkBeats(beats, { actions, gaits })`,
  `stickFigureTarget({ …, cast })` (draws the gaits, and lists the actions in
  `describeTarget`) and `handPath({ …, cast })`. Nothing is registered
  globally. Custom names may not reuse built-in ones, expanded beats are
  checked too, and nesting is capped.
- **`persona()`**: a character's look, height, acting style, gait, usual mood,
  stance, lip-sync energy and own actions and gaits, with `.figure()`,
  `.script()`, `.check()`, `.handPath()` and `.describe()`. Its unknown
  habits are rejected with suggestions.
- **`go`** beat action: walks to `to` in the persona's gait (`walk` without one).
- `scriptTracks` options `gait` (for `go`) and `rest` (the stance `stand`
  returns to).
- `capabilities(version, cast)` lists a cast's own actions and gaits, and the
  catalog documents the API for making your own.
- `stepsToKeys()`, shared by gags and step actions.
- Gallery card **Code Review**: two personas (a smug senior who struts, a
  nervous junior who sneaks) review TypeScript with a custom facepalm and a
  custom nod.

## Changed

- `resolveStickFrame` and `resolveStickPose` take an optional map of custom
  gaits.

## Testing

- Step actions splice in like gags; beats actions expand (passing on `at`,
  `mood`, `say`); custom gaits walk at their own pace and are drawn.
- The checks accept custom names, ask for the fields they need, and refuse
  shadowing, broken expansions and loops.
- Personas: figures, scripts (`go`, `stand`), descriptions, unknown habits.
- The catalog lists a cast's own actions and gaits.
