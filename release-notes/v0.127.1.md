# v0.127.1 — The Cosmic Dive example

Released: 2026-11-17

The cosmic explainer kit from v0.127.0 now has a live example on the
Examples page, so you can watch a dive through scale, pause it and scrub to
any moment, and copy the code into a page of your own.

## Added

- **Cosmic Dive** (`live-cosmic-dive`, in Video and Showcase): from a hand to
  skin, cells and one atom, each level opening as a growing portal in the
  middle of the last (`drawZoomLevels` with `depthAt`), over gradient skies,
  parallax dust and bokeh, flat-shaded spheres, a probability haze and an
  isometric cube, with label chips and burned-in captions two words at a
  time. Play or scrub; captions on or off.

## Docs

- docs/cosmic-explainer.md: when a level covers the frame,
  `portalRadius × factor^(depth − k) ≥ √(w² + h²) / 2` (depth `k + 0.28` with
  the defaults on a 1920×1080 frame); a smaller portal radius needs a deeper
  key, or the level behind shows at the corners.

## Validation

- The live-demo and copied-standalone-page tests run the example (it draws,
  animates, tears down cleanly and styles only its own classes).
- Frames rendered on a real canvas at eight scrub times and checked.
