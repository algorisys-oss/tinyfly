# v0.92.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-06

Characters come in builds, and the v2 human gets its own motion: a line of
action, gaits and gags posed in 3D, so they read from every view. The editor's
gags and walks now use them.

## Added

- **Builds in the editor**: the Character element's **Build** picker offers
  Standard, Slim, Kid (big head), Broad, Curvy and Stocky. **Head**,
  **Shoulders** and **Hips** sliders adjust each part, and one drag is one undo
  step (the Stick figure has a head size only). The build is saved as
  `CharacterElement.build` and shapes every preview and export.
  `CHARACTER_BUILDS`, `buildValue()` and `buildName()` are available for tools.
- **`bend` on the v2 human** (line of action): the back curves forward (+) or
  arches (−). The bend grows up the spine and carries on into the neck, where
  `lean` tips a straight back. It defaults to 0, so existing poses and drawings
  are unchanged. The acting pass leads with it.
- **Native v2 gaits**: `humanGaitPose(gait, phase, base)` and
  `humanGaitStrideLength(gait, height)`, driven by the same `GAITS` data as
  the stick figure. Walking is posed in the character's frame, so the side,
  side-left, back and ¾ views all read correctly with no mirroring.
- **Native v2 gags**: `HUMAN_GAGS` and `humanGag(name, { at, from, speed })`:
  take, double take, wind-up, land, tremble and deflate, written for the human
  body. A take flings the arms up in every view.

## Changed

- **The editor's gags and walks use the native v2 motion** in place of
  stick-figure poses converted with `stickToHuman`. `characterGagKeys` no
  longer takes `facing`, because the view carries it.
- **`stickToHuman`** maps the stick figure's `bend` to the human's `bend` in
  profile. Front-on it is still a sideways lean and head tilt.

## Testing

- Native motion tests:
  - `bend` curves the back and leaves the hips put;
  - every gait cycles, and its side-left view is the mirror image of its side
    view, limb for limb;
  - toes and the leading foot point the way the character walks;
  - every gag keeps its view and stays upright in four views.
- Build tests check that builds shape the character and that every build stands
  the same height.
- The orientation matrix now runs against the native motion.
