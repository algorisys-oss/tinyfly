# v0.76.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-02

Dressed, more natural stick figures. The stick figure now tells you where every joint is
without drawing it, and lets your own code draw costumes, hair and props at the right depth
inside it, so a shirt sits under the arms and hair over the head, and both follow every
pose, walk and squash. Figures can also turn toward where they are going, sit down on a seat
at a known height, and report which foot is on the ground. The default look is softer:
tapered limbs, rounded joints, hands and feet, and feet that stay on the ground. The 0.75
look is one option away (`classic: true`, checked pixel for pixel against golden frames).

## Added

- **An organic default look:** limbs taper, elbows and knees are always slightly rounded,
  small hands and feet (pointing the way the figure faces, tipping with the shin), the
  lower foot always rests on the ground (so a walk bobs), and the body leans into sitting
  down and standing up. `style.classic: true` draws exactly the 0.75 look.
- **`style.headSize`:** the head's diameter as a fraction of the height (default 0.24). A
  bigger head takes its room from the torso; the feet and hips stay put.
- **Face lines capped by head size:** with a thick `lineWidth` (the bold 4–5% look) the
  eyes, brows and mouth no longer blot together. The default brush is under the cap, so
  default faces are unchanged.
- **A more natural walk:** `walkPose` leans into the stride and swinging forearms follow
  through.
- **`taperedLine(ctx, points, from, to)`:** the tapered fill the limbs use, for sleeves and
  trouser legs.

- **`stickFigureJoints(pose, style)`:** the geometry `drawStickFigure` draws with: `hip`,
  `neck`, `shoulders`, `elbows`, `hands`, `knees`, `feet`, `toes`, `handAngle` (the
  forearm's direction, for holding props), `limbs` (each limb's centre line as drawn,
  curves included, and the `spine`) and `head` (`center`, `rx`, `ry`, `angle`, and `eyeY`, `browTopY`,
  `mouthY`, `faceX` in head units). Points are already mirrored for `facing`. Drawing and
  joints come from one rig, so they cannot disagree.
- **`style.layers`:** drawing hooks at fixed depths inside the figure: `behind` → legs →
  torso → `body` → back arm → `sleeve(back)` → front arm → `sleeve(front)` → `behindHead`
  → head and face → `overHead` → `front` → label. Hooks draw in the figure's space,
  unmirrored, with save/restore done for them, and get a sketch pen when the figure is
  sketched. The left arm is always the back arm.
- **`style.shoulderWidth`:** arms start from two shoulders, square to the spine, instead of
  one point (default 0).
- **`turn`** pose field (0 front-on → 1 profile): the face slides toward the facing side and
  narrows, and the shoulders close up.
- **`sit`** pose field (0..1): thighs level and forward, shins down, hips at
  `seatHeight(height)` above the feet; the lower foot stays on the ground while sitting
  down. `POSES.sit`.
- **Ground contact:** `feetY` and `grounded.left` / `grounded.right` in the joints.
- **`resolveStickPose(props, time)`**, **`stickFigureAt(target, frame, id)`** (a target's
  pose and scene-space joints in a frame, for `draw(ctx, frame)`), **`jointsToScene()`**,
  **`headPoint()`**, **`seatHeight()`**.
- **Examples:** a **Dressed Stick Figures** gallery card (Video category): a shirt, tapered
  trousers and sleeves, a pleated sari with its pallu, hair and a bun drawn with layers, a lota held at the hand's angle, a
  figure that walks in turned and sits on a charpai, and a speech bubble that follows a
  head, with a joints overlay toggle.

## Fixed

- **Headless video: custom targets drew at time 0.** `FrameRenderer` never plays its
  timeline, so the state's clock stayed at 0 and custom targets were always passed
  `time = 0`: a `talk` track did not move the mouth and sketched figures did not boil in
  `tinyfly video`. Custom targets now get the frame's time.

## Upgrade Notes

- **The default look changed.** Figures now draw in the organic look; add
  `style: { classic: true }` to keep the 0.75 drawing exactly. `walkPose` now also sets
  `lean`, `headTilt` and the swinging arms' elbows.
- **No breaking API changes otherwise.** `StickPose` gained `turn` and `sit`; a pose
  written without them draws front-on and standing, but TypeScript code that builds a full
  `StickPose` literal must add `turn: 0, sit: 0` (or build it with `pose()`).
- `stickFigureTarget` now returns a `StickFigureTarget` (a `CustomTarget` carrying its
  `figureStyle`), and its props include `turn` and `sit`.
- Headless videos with talking or sketched custom targets now animate them (see Fixed).
