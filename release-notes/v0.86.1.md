# v0.86.1

**Release Date:** 2026-10-05

A fix for ink outlines in 3D scenes drawn by the Canvas 2D renderer (the
renderer headless video uses).

## Fixed

- **Nicked outlines on subdivided meshes.** Each triangle's ink was drawn
  right after its fill, so the next neighbour's fill and seam stroke cut into
  it: tick marks every cell along rims and silhouettes (a ring's outer edge,
  a finely divided floor's rim). An edge's ink now waits until the faces of
  the same object at about its depth are painted, and goes down before
  anything of another object or clearly nearer, which may still cover it.
  Ink coverage along every edge of a test floor went from 90% to over 99.5%.

## Changed

- The scene-3d golden frames that use outlines are updated (cleaner edges).
- `todo.md` gains **Phase 35: Viral loops**: loop checks, nested zooms, a
  cartoon face with a pupil hook, bloom and light trails, 3D lines and a
  depth buffer for headless video, gooey shapes and beat-synced timing,
  from building the first viral loop videos with tinyfly.
