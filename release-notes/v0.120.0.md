# v0.120.0 — Characters present maths figures

Released: 2026-10-16

A maths figure can now be a surface, like a code panel, whiteboard or chart, so
a character can teach with it: walk up, point at a vector's tip or an equation
term, and change the figure as it speaks.

## Added

- `mathSurface(figure, { x, y })` in `@algorisys/tinyfly/math`, for
  `surfaceScript` from `@algorisys/tinyfly/characters`.
  - Anchors: `box`, `item:ID`, `tip:ID` (`tip:basis.i`), `point:ID`,
    `node:GRAPH:NODE`, `term:ID:TEXT` (`term:eq#2:x` for the second match) and
    `value:NAME`.
  - Edits: `drawOn`, `erase`, `show`, `hide`, `step` (equation steps) and `set`
    (tweens a number, a vector or a whole matrix).
  - Anchors follow the edits: after a `set` warps the plane, `tip:basis.i` is
    where î has gone.
  - Parts of a figure do not come loose: `piece`, `fling`, `follow` and `move`
    explain that edits are the way to change it.
- Gallery: **Maths Presenter**, a figure walking up to a linear map, pointing at
  ĵ as it slides into a shear, and at the square whose area stays 1.

## Notes

- The maths module still never imports the characters module. `mathSurface`
  has the shape of a characters `Surface` without depending on it, so the main
  browser bundle does not grow.

## Validation

- Anchors in scene px; terms found by their text, with occurrence counts.
- Edits written as tracks, with anchors moving as the matrix tweens.
- `surfaceScript` compiles beats against the surface; errors for unknown
  anchors, edits and values are tested.
- The demo was watched through a full loop in the app.
