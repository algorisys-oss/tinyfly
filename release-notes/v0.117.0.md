# v0.117.0 — Maths animations: probability and statistics

Released: 2026-10-16

`@algorisys/tinyfly/math` adds probability and statistics. Every random draw
comes from a seed, so a histogram fills the same way in a lesson page, a video
and an explorable.

## Added

- Distributions as data: `normal`, `uniform`, `exponential`, `binomial`,
  `poisson`, `dice`, and `mean` (the average of k draws of another).
  - `density`, `meanOf`, `sdOf`, `supportOf`, `sample` and seeded `samples`.
  - Figures may give a distribution's numbers as value names, so a slider or a
    timeline can move the mean or n.
- New figure items:
  - `histogram`: seeded samples, binned as a density or as counts. It fills as
    `count` grows and keeps the earlier samples, with an optional density
    curve.
  - `distribution`: a density curve, or bars for whole-number distributions,
    with a shaded P(a ≤ X ≤ b).
  - `galton`: a Galton board. Balls bounce by seeded coin flips and pile two to
    a row in their bins; the fractional part of `balls` is the next ball on its
    way down.
  - `scatter`: uniform points with an inside test (Monte Carlo), or noisy
    points about a line with a least-squares fit.
  - `bayes`: a unit square cut by a prior and a test's two error rates.
- Derived numbers for TeX:
  - `ID.p`, `ID.mean`, `ID.sd`
  - `ID.inside`, `ID.ratio`, `ID.slope`, `ID.intercept`, `ID.r`
  - `ID.posterior`, `ID.landed`
- Presets: `normalFigure`, `binomialFigure`, `cltFigure`, `galtonFigure`,
  `monteCarloPiFigure`, `regressionFigure`, `bayesFigure`.
- Engine: `randomNormal` (Box–Muller) and `shuffled` (Fisher–Yates) in the
  seeded random helpers.
- Gallery: Galton Board, Central Limit Theorem, Monte Carlo π, and Bayes: a
  Positive Test.
- Videos: `galton-short.mjs` (9:16) and `clt-explainer.mjs` (16:9).
- Docs: `docs/math.md`, the capability catalog and the model guides cover the
  new items.

## Validation

- Densities integrate (or sum) to 1 and match their stated means.
- Samples repeat for a seed and match their mean and spread; the averages of
  12 uniform draws narrow by 1/√12.
- Galton bins approach the binomial; least-squares fits recover their line;
  Bayes posteriors are checked by hand.
- Validation messages are tested.
- All gallery demos checked at desktop and phone widths; video stills
  reviewed.
