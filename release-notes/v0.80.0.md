# v0.80.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-05

The stick figure dances. Ten styles (disco, hip hop, breaking toprock, jazz,
K-pop, Bollywood, Bhangra, Bharatanatyam with mudras, Charleston and tap)
come as plain data counted in beats, so any tempo plays them and every pose
is a pure function of the beat. Flips, leaps and full splits join them. To
make it look like dancing, the figure gains wrists, ankles, feet that turn out
and in, hands with fingers, and a whole-body `spin` and `rise`.

## Added

- **New joints on the stick figure** (`StickPose`, all defaulting to 0, which
  draws exactly as before):
  - `leftWrist` / `rightWrist`: wrist bend; `handAngle` includes it, and the
    joints gain `fingertips`.
  - `leftAnkle` / `rightAnkle`: point the toe (tiptoe lifts the body) or flex
    onto the heel.
  - `leftFootOut` / `rightFootOut`: feet turned out or in (Bharatanatyam's
    turn-out, the Charleston's swivels).
  - `spin`: the whole body turned about the hips (a front flip forward, a
    cartwheel front-on); `rise`: lift off the ground, and once airborne the
    feet stop being planted, so a tuck keeps its height.
- **`style.hands`**: cartoon hands with fingers at the wrists, any `HandPose`
  per side, turned by the wrist.
- **Dance** (`src/characters/dance.ts`):
  - Moves keyed in beats (`DanceMove`, `DanceKey` with poses, hand shapes,
    easing), a `Groove` (bounce on or off the beat, sway), `DanceStyle`
    (stance, face, moves, routine), mirrored steps, half-beat blends.
  - `DANCE_STYLES`: `disco`, `hipHop`, `breaking`, `jazz`, `kpop`,
    `bollywood`, `bhangra`, `bharatanatyam`, `charleston`, `tap`.
  - `MUDRAS`: pataka, tripataka, alapadma, mushti, shikhara, hamsasya,
    katakamukha.
  - `danceFrame()`, `dancePose()`, `routineBeats()`, `beatAt()`,
    `mirrorPose()`, `applyGroove()`, `danceStance()`.
  - Foot strikes as data: `DanceKey.taps` and `danceTaps(style, from, to)`
    for tap sounds and stamps.
  - On a timeline: `stickFigureTarget({ dance: dancer(style) })` with `beat`
    and `dancing` props, `danceTracks()` (two tiny tracks) and
    `bakeDanceTracks()` (pose keyframes for timelines that stand alone as JSON).
- **Flips and leaps** (`src/characters/acrobatics.ts`): `FLIPS` (front flip,
  back flip, layout, scissor flip, side flip, cartwheel, back handspring,
  split leap, toe touch) as data: wind up, take off, a parabolic arc with the
  turn quickest at the top, land. `flipPose()`, `flipTravel()`, `flipTracks()`.
- **Full splits**: `POSES.sideSplit`, `POSES.frontSplit` (the hips settle on
  the floor), and jazz's `splitDrop` move.
- **Dance Floor (Characters)** gallery card: style, move and tempo pickers, a
  1–8 count light, flips blended in and out of the dance, tapping feet that
  flash, and optional Web Audio tap sounds.
- **Examples and docs:** `examples/headless-video/dance-sheet.mjs` (every move
  and flip, kept in `docs/model-sheet/dance-and-flips.png`) and
  [Dance and Flips](../docs/dance.md).
- **Planning:** [3D support plan](../docs/3d-support-plan.md) (todo Phase 33).

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking API changes.** New pose fields default to 0; dot hands and
  the classic look draw exactly as before. `StickFigureProps` gains optional
  `beat` and `dancing`.
- Not yet: floor work (windmills, headspins, freezes) and body rolls, which
  need Characters v2's spine and hand contact; a dance picker in the editor;
  hand shapes in baked tracks.
