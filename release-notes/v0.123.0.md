# v0.123.0 — Noir and detective scenes

Released: 2026-11-07

tinyfly now ships a kit for detective stories and film-noir moods on a
canvas, and the bean figures get household clothes.

## Added

- **Noir atmosphere** (`@algorisys/tinyfly/characters`), each a pure
  function of its options and the time (ms):
  - `drawRain(ctx, time, { x, y, width, height, drops, opacity, color, speed, length, slant, lineWidth, seed })`:
    streaks falling in a box, clipped to it, from a seeded generator.
  - `drawBlinds(ctx, { x, y, width, height, opacity, slope, spacing, band, color })`:
    key light through venetian blinds.
  - `drawDarkness(ctx, { amount, cones, holes, bounds, color, opacity, beam, beamOpacity })`:
    a dark overlay with torch beams (`{ x, y, angle (degrees), length, width }`)
    and round holes of light cut out; `coneCorners(cone)`.
  - `drawGlow`, `drawVignette`, `lightningFlash(time, strikes, decay)` and
    `lightFlicker(time, strength)`.
- **Detective overlays**: `drawClueMarker` (the red magnifier: pops on,
  pulses, fades; `clueMarkerAt`), `drawEvidenceFolder`, `drawClockFace`
  (`clockHandAngles`), `drawTimeChip`, `drawNameCard` (freeze-frame suspect
  card: the frame dims, a slanted red band beside the head, on the side with
  room), `drawNarrationBox` (first-person narration typed on a whole word at
  a time; `lineReveal`) and `drawSpeechBubble` (above the speaker, tail to the
  head, kept inside the frame). Text and font families are the caller's.
- **Case board**: `caseBoard({ x, y, width, height, cards, strings, marks, … })`
  checks and resolves a board (did-you-mean on unknown card ids and kinds);
  `drawCaseBoard(ctx, board, time, { drawCard })` draws the cork, photo and
  note cards popping up onto pins, red string stretching between them and
  the marker's `cross`, `strike`, `scribble` and `circle` marks drawn on over
  time, and returns the marker's nib while it draws
  (`caseBoardMarkerTip`). Also `caseCardBox`, `caseMarkPath`, `drawPushPin`
  and `drawMarkerStroke`.
- **Bean household clothes**: `bean.skirt` (a colour for a sari or long
  skirt, or `{ color, length }` for a kurta's hem; it flares standing and
  walking and lies along the thighs seated), `bean.drape` (a pallu or
  dupatta across the chest) and `bean.bangles`; the `beanSari` preset;
  helpers `beanAlongLeg`, `beanStanding` and `BEAN_BANGLE_STOPS`.
- `NOIR_KIT` in the capabilities catalog (`npx tinyfly capabilities`,
  `capabilities().noir`), a [Noir and Detective Scenes](../docs/noir-detective.md)
  guide, the appearance guide, the model guides and the
  [Noir Case File](https://tinyfly.app/examples/live-noir-case-file) live example.

## Changed

- Nothing for existing figures and scenes. A bean with a skirt draws its legs
  before the body (as under trousers), so the body covers the waist.

## Validation

- Unit tests: rain stays in its box, is the same at the same time, moves over
  time and changes with the seed; blinds bands, darkness with a beam and a
  hole, glow fall-off, vignette corners, lightning and flicker; the clue
  marker's window and ring, the clock's hands, the chip's width and fade, the
  name card's dimming and side, narration typing on, the speech bubble's
  placement and exit; the case board's defaults, did-you-mean errors, card
  geometry, pins popping up, strings stretching, cross strokes drawn in turn,
  the marker nib, `drawCard` clipping and determinism; the catalog.
- Bean: the order with a skirt, the skirt between and outside the legs and
  above the shoes, a kurta's shorter hem, the drape on the chest, bangles on
  both wrists, a seated skirt along the thighs, option checks and the preset.
- A full noir frame, the case board at three moments, and the sari and kurta
  figures standing, turned and seated were rendered and reviewed.
