# v0.37.0

**Release Date:** 2026-07-22

The camera gets **on-stage panning** and a **dedicated lane in the timeline** —
the camera now feels like a real authoring tool, not just raw keyframes.

## Highlights

- **✋ Pan on the stage.** With a camera on (DOM preview), click **✋ Pan** and drag
  the stage to pan the camera. The move is recorded as x/y keyframes **at the
  playhead** — scrub, drag, done. Panning is exact at any zoom or rotation.
- **🎥 Camera lane in the timeline.** Camera tracks float to the top of the
  timeline as a distinct, highlighted lane labelled 🎥 Camera, so pan/zoom/rotate
  keyframes are easy to find and edit.

## How it works

Dragging calls the same `setCameraValue` used by the Property Panel inspector, so
on-stage pan and numeric editing write identical keyframes. The pan gesture needs
no inverse transform: the camera's pan is applied in stage space **after** scale
and rotate, so a screen drag translates the framing 1:1 (converted to stage units
by the preview scale). Corner-zoom and a rotate handle stay numeric in the
inspector for now — those need the inverse transform and are a later refinement.

## Added

- **✋ Pan** toggle + drag overlay in the DOM preview (`preview-panel`).
- Camera tracks sorted to the top of the timeline with a 🎥 lane style
