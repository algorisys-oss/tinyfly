# v0.96.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-08

Figures act on more than code. The code panel's pattern of named places,
timed edits, loose pieces and floors that carry a figure is now a contract
any scene object can follow, called a *surface*. Two new surfaces join the
code panel: a whiteboard (or chalkboard) for lessons, and bar and line charts
for talking through data. A whole scene, with what the figure does and what
the surface does in answer, can now be written as plain JSON.

## Added

### Surfaces

- **The `Surface` contract**: a scene object whose places have names,
  `kind:args` (`line:7`, `token:4:Println`, `term:eq:+ 4`, `bar:q4`).
  `anchor(name, time?)` gives the box there, `piece(name)` makes the part
  there come loose, and `edit(name, anchor, options)` changes the surface at
  a time. A wrong name says what was probably meant
  (`Unknown anchor "lin": did you mean "line"?`).
- **The code panel is a surface**: `code.anchor('token:4:Println')`,
  `code.edit('fling', 'token:4:Println', { at })`, and every panel edit by
  name (highlight, strike, remove, type, insert, write, drop, move, fling).
  The existing methods are unchanged.
- **`surfaceScript(figureId, surfaces, beats, options)`**: beats that act on
  surfaces, as data. A beat's `target`, `to` and `onto` can name a place
  (`{ surface, anchor }`), and `then` says what the surface does in answer,
  at a moment of the beat (`at: 'start' | 'contact' | 'release' | 'end'`),
  optionally `until` a later moment. A `carry` cue makes a piece follow the
  figure's hand. Cues run in beat order, then the figure rides whatever it
  stands on. The Code Tidy demo written this way gives the same tracks, key
  for key.
- **`checkSurfaceBeats(beats, surfaces)`**: everything `checkBeats` finds,
  plus unknown surfaces, places, edits and moments, with suggestions.
- **Shared parts** in `src/characters/surface/`: `editLog`, `pieceMotion`,
  `rideFloors` and `surfaceBox`, for anyone building a surface of their own.
- **The catalog** lists every surface's anchors, edits and how to make one,
  plus the `surfaceScript` fields.

### Whiteboard

- **`whiteboard({ x, y, width, height, theme, items })`**: a board or
  chalkboard whose texts and hand-drawn marks (circle, box, underline, arrow)
  are declared as data. Text sits in fixed-width cells and each mark's wobble
  is seeded by its id, so a board is drawn the same everywhere.
- Places `text:ID`, `term:ID:TEXT` (part of a text, which can come loose) and
  `mark:ID`. Edits: `write` (a hidden text by hand, or new text into a term's
  place), `draw`, `erase`, `strike`, and `move` and `fling` for terms.
- **Board Lesson demo**: a figure solves 2x + 4 = 12 on a chalkboard.

### Charts

- **`chart({ x, y, width, height, kind: 'bar' | 'line', data, … })`**: bar
  and line charts with a round scale, light and dark themes, and value labels
  with prefix, suffix and decimals.
- Places `bar:ID` / `point:ID`, `label:ID` and `value:ID`, which follow the
  values over time. Edits: `set` (the value label counts along), `show`
  (grow a bar in, or draw the line out to a point) and `highlight`.
- **Bar tops are floors**: a figure standing on a bar is carried up and down
  as it grows and shrinks.
- **Chart Talk demo**: a figure points out a dip, leaps onto the Q4 bar and
  rides it up as Q4 takes off.

## Fixed

- **A figure that starts facing left and never turns** (`scriptTracks` with
  `facing: -1`) was drawn facing right, with its points and reaches mirrored:
  its one `facing` key was dropped. It is kept now.

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking changes.** `CodeBox`, `CodeEditOptions` and
  `CodeFlingOptions` are now aliases of the shared surface types, with the
  same fields.
- A beat script whose figure starts with `facing: -1` and never turns now
  has a `facing` track. If you drew such a figure facing left by hand to work
  around the bug, you can drop the workaround.
