# v0.81.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-05

Dancing in the editor, in time with the music. A 🧍 Character now dances any
of the ten styles and does every flip, written as ordinary keyframes from the
playhead. Music sets the beat: an Audio element's tempo is detected from the
audio (or tapped), the timeline shows its beats and bars, keyframes snap to
them, and dances start on the beat. Characters gain turned-out knees, foot
turn-out and hands that take shapes and mudras, so the dances look the same
on a character as on the stick figure.

v0.80.0 was tagged and published to the CDN but not to npm; this release
brings its dancing, flips and splits to npm too.

## Added

- **Beats** (engine, pure):
  - `BeatGrid` and `beatLength()`, `beatTime()`, `beatAtTime()`,
    `nearestBeat()`, `nextBeat()`, `beatsBetween()`.
  - `detectTempo(samples, sampleRate)`: tempo, first beat and confidence,
    deterministic (onset strength, autocorrelation with a gentle ~120 bpm
    prior, then tempo and phase fitted together over the clip).
- **Editor, Audio element → Beat**: 🎵 Detect tempo (decoded in the browser),
  tempo / first beat / beats per bar, 👆 tap tempo.
- **Editor, timeline**: beat lines behind every track, bars numbered in the
  ruler; dragged keyframes snap to beats (hold Alt to place freely).
- **Editor, Character → Dance**: style, move (or the whole routine) and tempo →
  🕺 Dance from playhead; a flip → 🤸 Flip at playhead. Keyframes merge around
  the character's existing keys. With music, **Follow beat** uses its tempo
  and starts on the nearest beat. Flips travel (an `x` track) the way the
  character faces.
- **Editor, Character → Hands**: Round, Cartoon gloves, or Natural (five
  fingers); dances key hand shapes and mudras; each finger is a track.
- **`stickToHuman(pose, hands?)`**: stick-figure poses on v2 characters (limb
  angles split by the view, legs turned out at the hip front-on, turn-out,
  hand shapes, wrists as hand roll, `rise` / `spin` as `lift` / `roll`).
- **v2 characters**: `leg.*.rotate` (turn out at the hip: knees bend out over
  the toes), `leg.*.toeOut` (foot turn-out), `handStyle: 'natural'`; hand
  fields labelled in plain words ("Right hand · index curl").
- **Stick figure**: hand shapes as `hand.left.*` / `hand.right.*` props on
  targets with `style.hands`, baked by `bakeDanceTracks()` (`hands: false` to
  skip).

## Changed

- **Tap** is danced side-on, so shuffles read as brushes forward and back.
- **`mirrorPose()` and the groove understand side-on poses**: mirrored, the
  other arm and leg do the move while the figure keeps facing the same way;
  both knees bounce forward.
- The examples test gives each model-sheet scene 20 s to draw.

## Technical Details

| File | Description |

| `src/characters/stick-to-human.ts` | Stick poses on v2 characters |

| `src/editor/utils/beat-grid.ts` | The scene's beat grid, tap tempo, decoding |
