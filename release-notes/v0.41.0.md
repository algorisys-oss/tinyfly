# v0.41.0

**Release Date:** 2026-07-22

**Rulers & draggable guides** — pull out your own guide lines and snap to them.

## Highlights

- **📏 Rulers.** Toggle rulers along the top and left of the stage (DOM preview).
- **Drag out guides.** Drag down from the top ruler for a vertical guide, right
  from the left ruler for a horizontal one.
- **Reposition & remove.** Guides are cyan and draggable; **drop one off the
  stage** to delete it.
- **Snap to guides.** While the rulers are shown, dragged and resized elements
  snap to your guides too — alongside the grid, other elements, and the artboard.

Guides are **editor-only** — never part of the animation or the saved JSON.

## How it works

Screen positions map to artboard coordinates through the canvas bounding rect, so
guide placement is correct at any preview scale. Guide positions feed straight
into the existing `snap.ts` static-line set, so snapping to a guide uses the same
tested math as snapping to an element edge.
