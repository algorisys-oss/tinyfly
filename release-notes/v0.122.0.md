# v0.122.0 — Bean figures

Released: 2026-11-06

tinyfly now draws the common white outlined stick-figure style natively, with
outlines that stay smooth at every joint.

## Added

- `figure: 'bean'`: a white figure with a thick black outline, smooth noodle
  limbs, a shaped body (rounded shoulders, a slight waist, fuller hips and a
  rounded seat), a round head with big oval eyes, white mittens and cartoon
  shoes (a flat sole on the ground, a rounded toe cap, a low heel and a grey
  sole strip). The rig solves the pose as for every figure, so every pose,
  gait, dance, reach, turn and lip-sync works.
  - Each limb is one curve stroked in the ink, then in the fill: no seams at
    the elbows or knees. The front arm, and on a plain figure the neck and the
    legs, join the body without a seam; under a tee or trousers the hem shows.
  - Painter order: back arm, legs, neck, body, front arm, head; a limb
    reaching toward the viewer (seated thighs, hands on the knees) draws over
    the body.
  - `bean: { limbs: 'tube' | 'line', torso, limbWidth, lineWidth, outlineWidth, fill, shirt, sleeves, trousers, shoes }`.
    `limbs: 'line'` is the bare stick variant (round-capped ink lines, with an
    optional pale `halo` edge). The near arm is in front of the body in both
    modes. A dark `shoes` colour gets a shine. An `outfit`'s shirt, trousers
    and sleeves colour it too.
  - Detective gear: `coat` (a trench coat with lapels, belt and buckle,
    buttons, a knee hem and sleeves to the wrist), `fedora` (brim, pinched
    crown, band) and `shades` (big dark aviators in place of the eyes, the
    brows still showing; `face.shades` on any oval-eyed face).
- `face.style: 'oval'` (any figure): big white oval eyes with small pupils
  following the gaze, skin-coloured lids for `lid`, blinks and half-open eyes,
  bigger eyes for `eye.*` above 1 and `bulge`, and heavy brows for `brow.*`
  and `browTilt`. Glasses fit round them.
- `hands: 'mitten'` (any figure): a plain oval mitten along the forearm.
- `BEAN_CAST` presets (also in `CHARACTER_CAST`, group `bean`): `beanMan`,
  `beanWoman`, `beanKid`, `beanBoss`, `beanDetective`, `beanStick`.
- Helpers: `resolveBean`, `beanTorso`, `beanTorsoOutline`, `beanLimbLine`,
  `beanOrder`, `beanOverBody`, `mittenAt`, `beanShoeAt`, `drawBeanFedora`.
- Editor: Figure "Bean (white outlined)" with Limbs and Body fullness
  controls, Face style "Big oval eyes" and Hands "Mittens".
- Capabilities catalog, `llms-full.txt`, the appearance guide and the model
  guides list the new names.

## Changed

- Nothing for existing figures: their drawing, joints and order are
  unchanged (snapshot and pixel tests unchanged).

## Validation

- Unit tests: resolved defaults and option checks; joints identical to the
  plain rig; painter order for tube, line, dressed, profile and seated poses;
  the body's shape; pixel checks that limbs, the neck, the hips and the front
  shoulder join have no inner seam and that every limb section is closed by
  ink on both sides; line limbs, shoes, clothes colours; oval-eye fields,
  expressions and glasses; mittens on the fluid figure; presets and catalog.
- Front, three-quarter, profile, back-turned, waving, pointing, seated,
  hands-on-hips and dance frames, a head sheet of every expression with
  glasses and facial hair, and the preset cast were rendered and reviewed.
