# v0.82.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-05

Glides and the moonwalk. Dances can now travel across the floor: a move says
how far one loop carries the figure, and it goes at a steady speed matched to
the planted foot, so that foot stays put while the body glides over it. A new
Popping style has a side glide, the moonwalk, a forward glide and a toe stand.
In the editor, a character facing left dances the mirror image, and the
gallery's live demos no longer cut off their controls.

## Added

- **Travelling dances** (characters, pure):
  - `DanceMove.travel`: ground one loop covers, in figure heights (positive
    toward +x; mirrored front-on it goes the other way, side-on it keeps its
    direction).
  - `danceTravel(style, beat, options?)`: ground covered at a beat.
  - `danceTravelTrack(target, style, { height, x?, bpm?, beats?, start?, fade?, move?, mirror? })`:
    an `x` track with linear keys where the speed changes; it eases in and
    out over `danceTracks()`' `fade` (default 1 beat).
  - `bakeDanceTracks({ height, x })` adds the `x` track itself.
- **Popping** in `DANCE_STYLES`: side glide (front-on), moonwalk and forward
  glide (side-on), toe stand. The routine comes back to where it started, so
  it loops on the spot. Tests check the planted foot stays within 2% of the
  height while the other slides, both on the floor.
- **`mirrorHumanPose(pose)`**: a v2 pose's mirror image (sides swap; turn,
  sideways lean, head turn and tilt, roll and look reverse), checked pixel for
  pixel against a flipped drawing.
- **Editor, Character → Dance**: glides and the moonwalk write an `x` track
  from where the character stands; facing left (Side (left) view), it dances
  the mirror image and glides the other way, turned between 3 and 4 like flips.
- **Dance Floor**: the dancer travels with the glides and wraps round the stage.

## Changed

- Dances and flips in the editor read which way the character faces from the
  timeline at the playhead (`facingOf(turn)`), not only its base pose.
- The dance model sheet (`docs/model-sheet/dance-and-flips.png`) has a Popping row.

## Fixed

- **Gallery live demos showed only part of their controls**: a card's live
  preview now grows to fit its pickers, sliders and buttons (180px is the
  minimum, not the size).
- **Demos restyled each other**: Dance Floor and Dressed Stick Figures shared
  `df-*` class names (and `df-wrap`, `im-hint`, `sg-grid` were shared too), so
  one demo's styles changed the other's canvas. Renamed; a test fails when two
  demos style the same class.
