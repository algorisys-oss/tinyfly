# v0.107.1 — Softer angular faces and fitted hair

Released: 2026-10-12

The Flowing Cartoon Hair example's soft angular face has fuller cheeks, a
wider chin and rounded jaw transitions. Hair meets the actual face outline
without the white crescent left by an oval clipping mask.

## Fixed

- Soften the soft angular demo preset with a wider chin and rounded corners.
- Clip behind-head hair, ears and beards to the projected angular outline in
  close-ups, full characters and projected scene views.
- Preserve existing sharp jaw outlines by default. Plain JSON
  `face.jawRoundness` (0–1) opts into rounding in all drawing looks.

## Validation

- Check corner smoothing and deterministic JSON geometry across eight views.
- Pixel regressions cover hair beside both sharp and rounded angular chins.
- Desktop/mobile and standalone checks cover face and hairstyle controls.
- Full unit suite, production build and public type declarations pass.

## Upgrade Notes

- Existing round faces and sharp manga outlines keep their shape.
- Set `face: { shape: 'angular', jawRoundness: 1 }` to soften jaw corners;
  adjust `jawWidth` and `chinWidth` independently.
