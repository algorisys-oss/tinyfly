# v0.115.0 — Illustrated character look

Released: 2026-10-15

Characters can be drawn the way an illustrator inks and paints them: line
weight that follows the light, strokes that taper like a brush, and fills
painted with shadow and highlight tones mixed from their own colour.

## Added

- `look: 'illustrated'` for characters. Outlines thicken on the side turned
  away from the light and thin on the lit side; open strokes (brows, mouth
  lines, folds, hair strands) taper at their ends. Skin, clothes and hair get
  graded shadow, core-shadow and highlight bands.
- `illustrated: { light, weight, taper, shadow, highlight }` tunes the look;
  `light` points toward the light in screen space and defaults to the body's
  `shading.light`, the rest run 0..1. `resolveIllustration` checks and fills
  in the options.
- `illustrationTones(colour)`: a fill's shadow, core-shadow and highlight
  tones. Shadows lean toward red-violet, highlights toward warm yellow, greys
  shade cool; translucent fills stay unpainted.
- Painted faces in the illustrated look: heavier arched brows, a lid shadow
  and crease, an iris glow, a soft lower lip and cheek warmth.
- **Illustrated Look** gallery demo: clean and illustrated side by side, with
  light direction (or a circling light), weight, taper, shadow and highlight
  controls. The Cast Turnaround and Flowing Hair demos offer the look too.

## Changed

- Hair `shine` is drawn in the illustrated look as well as the clean one.
- `Pen.line` takes an optional `outside` side and `Pen.shape` an optional
  `flat` flag; existing pens ignore both, so custom layers are unaffected.

## Validation

- All 3,853 tests across 241 files pass, including new tone, option,
  determinism, light/option sensitivity and layer-hook tests and the new
  demo's gallery and standalone-page runs.
- Clean and illustrated casts and head close-ups compared side by side; the
  demo checked in the app at desktop and phone widths.
