# v0.105.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-12

Original young adult manga characters with editable angular faces and pointed
hair, plus adjustable arm proportions for the original stick figure.

## Added

- `MANGA_CAST`: `mangaYoungMan`, `mangaYoungWoman`, `mangaRival` and
  `mangaHeroine`, also available through `CHARACTER_CAST` and `castMember`.
- `FaceOptions.shape`, `jawWidth` and `chinWidth`: a projected tapered pen
  head that follows yaw, nod and roll. Anime `eyeScale` and `eyeAspect`
  customise eye proportions while retaining expressions, blink and gaze.
- `mangaSpikes` / `mangaSweep` hairstyles and editable `HairSpike` tufts
  in `hair.spikes`, projected through head turns. Public `MangaCastName` type.
- Character editor jaw, chin, eye, nose and line-width controls; manga cast
  choices in both editor pickers. Custom hair fields survive scene JSON;
  choosing a different hairstyle clears the preceding hair overrides.
- Young Adult Manga Cast website example with editable hair, face shape,
  proportions, expressions, whole figures, close-ups and full turnarounds.
- `StickStyle.armLength`, a positive finite multiplier (default 1) of both
  upper arms and forearms; hands and other body proportions retain their sizes.
  The existing Stick Figure example adds an arm-length slider.

## Validation

- Tests cover jaw/chin geometry, projected turns with nod and roll, all four
  presets as JSON in every pen look, appearance overrides and shared expressions.
- Arm-proportion regressions cover mirroring, classic and organic looks,
  squash/stretch, scene wrists, unchanged hands/body and invalid multipliers.
- Existing anime/simple faces retain their default drawing.
- Browser checks cover hair/face changes, expressions, turnarounds and the
  exported standalone page.

## Upgrade Notes

- Defaults remain round heads and original arm proportions. The manga presets
  are original archetypes; hair, clothing, skin and face details stay editable.
- The angular outline applies to pen characters. Existing contact head bounds
  and solid mesh heads retain their geometry.
- Nested `castMember` overrides replace option objects; spread the preset face
  to retain its other fields. The original stick figure's arm setting is a style
  option, separate from the newer human rig's `CharacterOptions.armLength`.
