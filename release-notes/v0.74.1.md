# v0.74.1

**Release Date:** 2026-09-27

The stick figure walks properly. Its legs now take turns stepping forward and back instead of
swinging together, and in the examples its feet stay planted on the ground.

## Fixed

- **Walk cycle (`walkPose`):** both legs, and both arms, swung the same way on screen.
  Limb angles are measured outward from each side, so a change that moves the left limb
  one way moves the right limb the other. Now:
  - the legs scissor evenly about the vertical, one foot forward while the other is back;
  - the leg swinging forward lifts, its shin trailing backward (the left shin used to fold
    forward);
  - hanging arms swing against the legs; raised arms (a wave, a point) and the face keep
    their pose.
- **Sliding feet in the examples:** the walk ran at a fixed pace while the figure eased in
  and out, so the feet skated. The video example and the Narrated Scene card now take the
  walk phase from the distance covered, and the card fades the walk in and out instead of
  snapping to standing.

## Added

- `strideLength(height, stride?)`: the ground one walk cycle covers. Set `walk` to
  distance / stride length, with the same easing as `x`, to keep the feet planted.

## Technical Details

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|

| `examples/headless-video/stick-figure.mjs` | Walk track from distance, same easing as `x` |
| `src/examples/live-demos/narrated-scene.js` | Walk phase from distance, fade in and out |

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking API changes.** A walking figure looks different, as intended. Leg angles
  during a walk no longer depend on the base pose's hip splay.
