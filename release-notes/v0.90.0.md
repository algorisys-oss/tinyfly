# v0.90.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-06

Story tools for cartoon acting. A story can now be written as beats ("walk
there, look, do a take, say this, run off") and compiled into acted tracks:
- a curved line of action;
- walks with personality;
- lip-sync from text, in English or Hindi;
- camera shots: push-ins, cuts, shakes and follows.

See [docs/acting.md](../docs/acting.md).

## Added

- **Line of action: `bend`** (stick figure pose field). It curves the spine
  as an arc from the hips, degrees from hips to neck: positive curls forward,
  negative arches back. The chest, arms and head ride on its end. It blends,
  keys and acts like any joint, and the gags use it. At 0 the figure is drawn
  exactly as before. `stickToHuman` maps it to lean and head tilt.
- **Gaits as data: `GAITS`**: `walk` (the same as `walkPose()`), `bouncy`,
  `doubleBounce`, `sneak`, `strut`, `tired` and `run`.
  - `gaitPose(gait, phase, base, stride)` gives the pose at a phase of the
    cycle.
  - `gaitStrideLength(gait, height)` gives the ground covered per cycle, so
    feet stay planted.
  - On `stickFigureTarget`, a `gait` prop picks the gait (a string track
    switches it), and `facing` can now be animated.
- **Lip-sync from text**:
  - `soundsOf(text)` reads a line as mouth sounds, and `VISEMES` holds the
    shapes.
  - `lipSyncKeyframes(line)` and `lipSyncTracks(target, lines)` write
    `mouth` and `mouthWidth` keyframes.
  - `lipSyncOver(target, tracks, lines)` lip-syncs over acted tracks, so the
    face carries on around each line.
  
  It reads Latin script and Devanagari (inherent vowels, matras, virama; lip
  consonants close the mouth).
- **Beat scripts: `scriptTracks(target, beats, options)`**. A beat can be:
  - a gait with `to`: walks there at the gait's pace, turning round first if
    the place is behind;
  - a named pose, or `stand`;
  - a gag;
  - `look` or `face`, with `toward`;
  - `say`: lip-synced, with small head nods;
  - `hold`.
  
  Any beat also takes `mood`, `say` and `pose`. Returns the acted tracks
  plus `x`, `walk`, `walking`, `gait` and `facing` tracks, the spoken lines
  with their times, and when each beat starts and ends. `speechDuration()`
  and `GAIT_CYCLE_MS` give the default timings.
- **Camera**:
  - `cameraTracks(shots, { stage })` (engine) compiles shots into tracks on
    the `Camera` target. A `frame` shot pushes in, pulls out or cuts when its
    duration is 0; a `shake` dies away over its duration and is seeded; a
    `follow` keeps a subject's x centred, a lag behind it.
  - `applyCamera()`, `cameraFromValues()`, `cameraPoint()` and
    `IDENTITY_CAMERA` (canvas adapter and browser bundle) apply a view to a
    canvas, or say where a scene point lands on screen.
  - In video scenes, `camera: true` films the scene through the `Camera`
    target, and a new `overlay` step draws captions and titles in screen
    space, unmoved by the camera.
- **Examples**:
  - `examples/headless-video/beat-script.mjs`: a beat script filmed by
    camera shots.
  - The Hindi pencil story (`examples/pencil-story-hindi/`), re-timed with
    acting: a take, a double take, tired and bouncy walks, a lip-synced line
    and camera pushes. The animator's hand is mapped through the camera, so
    it stays on what it draws.
  - Website gallery card **Cartoon Acting**: the script live in the browser,
    with pickers for the acting style and the walk, and toggles for drawing on
    twos, speed lines and the camera.

## Changed

- The acting pass limits each move's overlap delay to half the time between
  its two keys. Before, every move was limited by the shortest gap anywhere
  in the scene, so one quick key switched overlap off everywhere.

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking API changes.**
- `StickPose` gains `bend` (default 0). Code that builds a full `StickPose`
  object by hand, without `pose()` or `REST_POSE`, needs to add it.
- `stickFigureTarget` props gain `gait` (a string) and `facing`. Code that
  loops over the props assuming they are all numbers should skip `gait`.
