# v0.35.1

**Release Date:** 2026-07-22

## Added

- **Camera demo samples.** A new **Camera** category in the Samples dialog with
  three one-click demos that show off the camera:
  - **Push In** — a cinematic zoom into a title.
  - **Pan Across** — the camera pans across a row of cards.
  - **Orbit Reveal** — rotate and zoom out to reveal an emblem.

  They're ordinary data-authored samples (camera = tracks targeting the reserved
  `Camera` target), so they play in the editor and export/embed like anything else.
