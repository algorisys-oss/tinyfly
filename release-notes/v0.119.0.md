# v0.119.0 — Maths animations: AI/ML

Released: 2026-10-16

`@algorisys/tinyfly/math` adds machine-learning figures: networks whose
activations flow, gradient descent over a contoured loss surface, attention
heatmaps, a classifier finding its decision boundary, and k-means. Every
figure is seeded and computed exactly, so it trains the same way every time
it plays.

## Added

- `network` item: a fully connected network with seeded weights. Blue edges
  add, red ones subtract, and thicker edges count more. `flow` moves
  activations layer by layer, with pulses on the edges being fed. Inputs can be
  values, and the outputs are derived numbers (`ID.out0` …).
- `heatmap` item: given cells (with per-row softmax for attention, token
  labels and values written in), or a function of x and y. Colormaps:
  `viridis`, `heat`, `diverging`.
- `contours` item: level curves of f(x, y) by marching squares, joined into
  whole curves.
- `descent` item: gradient descent with exact partial derivatives and optional
  momentum. Diverging steps stop cleanly. Derived numbers: `ID.loss`, `ID.x`,
  `ID.y`, `ID.step`.
- `classifier` item: logistic regression on seeded clusters, trained epoch by
  epoch from a deliberately wrong line. Shows the probability field.
  Derived numbers: `ID.accuracy`, `ID.loss`.
- `kmeans` item: Lloyd's algorithm from the points nearest the middle of the
  data, with centroid trails and fractional iterations. Derived number:
  `ID.inertia`.
- Presets: `neuralNetworkFigure`, `gradientDescentFigure`, `attentionFigure`,
  `decisionBoundaryFigure`, `kmeansFigure`, `activationFigure`.
- The pieces on their own: `seededNetwork`, `forward`, `descentPath`,
  `trainLogistic`, `kmeansHistory`, `contourLines`, `colorAt`, `blendOver`.
- Gallery: Neural Network, Gradient Descent, Decision Boundary, K-Means,
  Attention Weights.
- Videos: `gradient-descent-short.mjs` (9:16) and
  `neural-network-explainer.mjs` (16:9).

## Changed

- TeX's `\color{name}` uses the figure theme's colours (`blue` is the theme's
  blue).
- The TeX subset knows `\tanh`, `\sinh`, `\cosh`, `\sec`, `\csc`, `\cot`,
  `\arcsin`, `\arccos`, `\arctan`, `\gcd`, `\ker`, `\dim` and `\softmax`.

## Validation

- Network weights and forward passes repeat for a seed.
- Contours trace a circle as one closed curve, including where grid samples
  sit exactly on the level.
- Descent reaches the minimum with the loss falling each step; diverging rates
  stop cleanly.
- Logistic regression reaches over 97% accuracy on separable clusters, and
  k-means lowers its inertia to three clusters.
- Attention rows sum to 1.
- All gallery demos checked at desktop and phone widths; video stills
  reviewed.
