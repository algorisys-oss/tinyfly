# v0.108.0 — Soft cartoon stories, children and editable clothing

Released: 2026-10-13

Create original adult and child story characters from the same public rig.
The Soft Cartoon Characters example lets you choose adults, children or an
adult and child, then their face, hairstyle, clothing and action.

## Added

- `figure: 'organic'`: filled, shaped anatomy with JSON width profiles,
  independent ink settings, shared skin colour and deterministic cel shading.
- `softCartoonCharacter()` and `SOFT_CARTOON_CAST`: editable `youngMan`,
  `youngWoman`, `childBoy` and `childGirl` presets. Children use larger head
  proportions, shorter limbs, rounded torsos and smaller default heights.
- Anatomy-fitted everyday and optional leaf garments, small articulated
  hands, editor support, headless contact sheets and a story motion example.
- Interactive adult/child choices with seven actions, flowing hair, face,
  clothing, skin, outline, shading, playback and time controls.

- `outfitPreset()` and `OUTFIT_PRESETS`: Everyday, Formal and Casual wardrobe
  defaults as JSON. Editable shirts, T-shirts, blouses, dresses, trousers,
  jeans/cargo details, shorts, skirts, sleeves, colours, collars and ties.
- Independent outfit editing for either example actor, plus editor presets
  and saved garment choices. All garments support adults and children.

## Fixed

- Cover rounded sleeve roots and waist attachments without clothing gaps.
- Move organic eyes/mouth with the head, retain both eyes in three-quarter
  views, narrow the far eye toward profile and hide features behind opaque hair.
- Use a flatter facial plane in profile with a small forward nose above the
  mouth, without pushing the chin forward.
- Keep foreground organic arms and hands together over waist garments,
  including global 3D scene depth sorting with separate world-object occlusion.
- Limit garment depth promotion to foreground arms; leaning head, neck,
  torso and legs retain their physical world-object occlusion.
- Render transparent organic skin as outlines across body, head, dot hands
  and articulated hands in every look, without skin fill or shading.
- Preserve visible face continuity as existing characters pass either profile.

- Preserve inherited opacity in body/head/hand shading.
- Apply scene lighting and fog consistently to organic body, face and hands.
- Snapshot and validate leaf options at construction; snapshot basic garments.
- Reject inherited object keys as unknown soft cartoon actor names.
- Reuse each head contour for filling, shading and face clipping within a draw.

- Resolve editor appearance defaults from the rendered figure, including
  organic proportions, natural hands, anime eyes, iris colour and outline width.
- Resolve and edit imported body fill/outline colours through Skin and Line
  controls while preserving unrelated options and source JSON ownership.
- Use camera-relative body direction for garment details while preserving
  authored turns and independent head yaw.

## Upgrade Notes

- Existing fluid/stick defaults remain available. Organic anatomy opts in
  through `figure: 'organic'` or `softCartoonCharacter(name, options)`.
- Use `softCartoonCharacter('childBoy', { height: 260 })` or `childGirl` for
  children. Height scales the actor while keeping the preset proportions.
- Organic faces default to `face.projection: 'volume'`; `cartoon` keeps the
  classic face slide. Profiles, materials and garments are plain JSON.
