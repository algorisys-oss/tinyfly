# v0.70.2

**Release Date:** 2026-09-19

The step buttons on teaching figures now look like a pair.

## Fixed

### Step button icons

- **What was wrong:** the step controls were `◀` and `▶|`. `◀` read as rewind sitting next
  to play, and `▶|` as skip-to-end, so the two did not look like one control.
- **Now:** they are `|◀` and `▶|`, mirror images of each other. Labels, behaviour and the
  arrow-key bindings are unchanged.

## Changed

- **README:** a "Publishing to npm" section under Development: `npm run release:npm`, what
  `prepublishOnly` runs, and why the version must be bumped first.
