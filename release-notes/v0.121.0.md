# v0.121.0 — A loss surface in 3D

Released: 2026-10-16

Gradient descent can now be shown in 3D: a ball rolling down a loss surface
along the exact-gradient path, with the camera turning round it.

## Added

- `lossSurface3D({ expr, start, rate, momentum?, steps?, stepDuration?, region?, resolution?, height?, orbit?, colormap? })`
  in `@algorisys/tinyfly/math`. It builds f(x, y) as scene JSON for
  `@algorisys/tinyfly/scene-3d` and returns `{ scene, tracks, duration, path, losses }`:
  - a mesh coloured by height;
  - the descent path, one piece per step that follows the surface and appears
    as the ball passes;
  - a ball keyed along the surface;
  - a slow camera orbit.
- The maths module still does not import scene-3d: the scene is plain data in
  its format.
- Gallery: **Loss Surface in 3D**, with a choice of momentum, a plain step, or a
  zigzag.
- Video: `loss-surface-3d.mjs` (16:9).

## Changed

- `colorAt` returns hex (`#440154`) rather than `rgb(…)`, so the same
  colours work in 2D, SVG and scene-3d mesh colours.

## Validation

- The scene loads in scene-3d, with mesh sizes, hex colours and one path piece
  per step checked.
- The ball moves downhill, the path appears behind it, and the build is the
  same every time.
- The demo was checked in the app at desktop and phone widths; frames were
  rendered and reviewed.
