# v0.46.0

**Release Date:** 2026-07-31

Scene duration is now authorable and self-correcting — and the timeline ruler
finally lines up with the keyframes.

## Highlights

### Editable scene duration

The `current / duration` readout in the playback bar was display-only, and the
only thing in the whole editor that could change a scene's duration was the
typewriter preset. Now the duration is an **editable field** (in seconds): click
it, type a length, press Enter. <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels, and the change is a
normal undoable edit.

Every scene keeps its own duration (new scenes still start at 2s), and it is the
hard end of the animation: playback stops there, the playhead cannot scrub past
it, and every exporter samples `0 → duration`.

### The timeline grows to fit your keyframes

Previously, dragging a keyframe past the end of the scene silently parked it
somewhere unreachable — the duration never moved, so the keyframe never played
and never exported. A track whose keyframes all sat past the end simply looked
frozen; one with a single keyframe pushed out got its motion truncated mid-ease.

Any edit that adds or moves a keyframe past the end now **extends the duration to
cover it**: keyframe drags, double-click-to-add, paste, presets, staggered
presets, motion paths, camera keyframes, and track batches. It only ever grows —
shortening stays deliberate.

### Trimming, and the Fit button

Shortening the duration below your last keyframe is still allowed (parking
keyframes past the end is a legitimate way to stash work), but it's no longer
invisible:

- The duration turns **amber** while keyframes sit past the end.
- A **Fit** button appears next to it — one click snaps the duration back out to
  the last keyframe.
- The playhead is pulled back inside the new range, so the readout can never show
  an unreachable time.

### Ruler alignment fix

The dope-sheet and curve-editor rulers were offset from the keyframe lanes by the
width of the track-label column (120px). Ticks, the playhead, and keyframes never
lined up: a keyframe at 2s was drawn 1.2s to the right of the ruler's `2s` mark.
Ruler clicks and track double-clicks inherited the same offset, so scrubbing and
adding a keyframe both landed at the wrong time.

The ruler now sits in its own lane behind a gutter that matches the label column,
so ruler, playhead, and keyframes share one origin. Click-to-seek and
double-click-to-add-keyframe are bound to the lane and map to the time under the
cursor. The gutter tracks the label column across the mobile breakpoints, and
box-select measures the lane offset instead of assuming 120px.

### End-of-scene marker

The ruler always draws at least 5 seconds regardless of the scene length, which
made it impossible to see where playback actually stops. There is now a dashed
amber line at the duration, and the region past it is dimmed in every track lane.

## Upgrade Notes

Upgrade from v0.45.4:

1. No API changes. `Timeline.setDuration()` and `config.duration` behave exactly
   as before — the new behaviour lives in the editor store.
2. Existing projects are untouched on load. The first keyframe edit that reaches
   past a scene's stored duration will extend it, which is the intended fix; use
   **Fit** or type a duration if you want a different length.
