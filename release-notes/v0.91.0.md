# v0.91.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-06

Cartoon acting comes to the editor. A Character element can now be acted in
a style, given gags, walked in a gait and given lines to say, without code.
Drawing on twos becomes a timeline setting that every player honours. Also
new: the cartoon zip in beat scripts, and a test matrix that checks
characters of every build and outfit stay oriented through all of it.

## Added

- **Editor: an Acting section on the Character element**:
  - Pick a style and click **🎭 Act the keyed poses**. The element keeps its
    plain key poses (`acting`) and its tracks are generated from them:
    - keying a pose, adding a gag, a walk or a line re-acts the whole
      performance;
    - changing the style re-acts it in the new style;
    - turning acting off restores the plain poses.
    
    Each action is one undo step.
  - **Gag at playhead**: take, double take, wind-up, land, tremble or deflate,
    in the character's current view.
  - **Walk left / right**: any gait for a distance, side-on, with the feet
    planted and an `x` track.
  - **Say at playhead**: lip-syncs a typed line (English or Hindi).
  - **Drawing**: every frame, on twos or on threes.
- **`TimelineConfig.drawingRate`** (engine): drawings per second. Every value
  holds on its drawing (`getStateAtTime` uses `heldTime`), so previews,
  players, embeds and exports all step the same way. It is saved in the JSON,
  and set with `timeline.drawingRate = 12`.
- **The cartoon zip**: `{ do: 'zip', to }` in `scriptTracks`. The figure winds
  up, its legs wheel in place, and it is gone, leaving its dust hanging.
- **Script effect cues**: `ScriptResult.effects` gives the time, scene x and
  length of the dust where a take lands, where a `land` gag lands and where a
  zip leaves, ready for `drawDustPuff`.
- **Camera helpers from `@algorisys/tinyfly/characters`**: `applyCamera`,
  `cameraFromValues` and `cameraPoint` are now exported where story scenes
  import from.
- Editor utilities (`src/editor/utils/character-acting.ts`):
  - `keyPosesOf`, `actingTracks`, `withRemovals`, `spliceKeys`, `upsertKey`:
    key poses in and out of a character's tracks;
  - `characterGagKeys`, `characterWalk`: gags and walks as key poses;
  - `stickTurnOf`: maps a character's view onto the stick figure's turn.
- `replaceTracks(target, tracks, record)` in the editor store removes a
  property given no keyframes, and can join the undo step just taken.
  `setDrawingRate(rate)` is new.

## Fixed

- **A front-on flip facing left (a cartwheel, a side flip) is now its mirror
  image.** It used to step off one way and wheel the other.

## Testing

- `character-orientation.test.ts` checks that characters stay the right way
  up and face the right way through acting, gags, walks, dances and flips:
  - 5 builds (bold, thin, big head, broad, tall);
  - every gait in both directions;
  - every gag in front, side and side-left views (a take's arms go up in each);
  - every dance and flip facing both ways (left is right's mirror image);
  - acting in each view;
  - every figure × look × hand style × outfit draws them.
- Stick figure gaits face the way the figure faces in every style (classic,
  rubber, big head and shoulders).

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking API changes.**
- `CharacterElement` gains an optional `acting` field (saved with the project).
- A timeline with `drawingRate` steps its values in every player. Leave it
  unset for smooth animation.
