# v0.79.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-04

Cartoon hands. `@algorisys/tinyfly/characters` gains a hand you can pose: a
palm and five fingers built in 3D and drawn flat, so turning the wrist from
the back of the hand to the palm needs no extra drawing. Fourteen ready-made
shapes go from a fist to a pencil grip, and every field of a hand pose is a
number that blends and animates. The animator's drawing hand is rebuilt on it,
holding the pencil in a real writing grip, and a new `handAt()` moves one hand
through a whole scene, gliding between strokes instead of popping in and out.

## Added

- **Cartoon hands** (`src/characters/hands/`):
  - `handJoints(pose, style)`: every finger's joints, depths and widths, the
    palm outline, which way the palm faces, and the hand's axes on screen.
  - `drawCartoonHand(ctx, wrist, pose, style, time)`: drawn through a look's
    pen (clean, pencil, silhouette), parts far to near, nails where they face
    you, knuckles on a curled back of hand. Options: `size`, `side` (left is
    the right mirrored), `angle`, `fingers: 4 | 5`, `plump` (cartoon gloves),
    `skin`, `ink`, `pen` (match a character), and a held `prop` drawn among the
    fingers at its depth.
  - Pose fields: `<finger>.curl`, `thumb.curl`, `thumb.across`, `spread`,
    `turn`, `bend`, `tilt`, `roll`. `handPose()`, `HAND_REST`, `mixHandPoses()`.
  - `HAND_SHAPES`: relaxed, open, spread, flat, fist, point, thumbsUp, peace,
    ok, pinch, cupped, wave, pencilGrip, hold.
- **`handAt(strokes, time, options)`**: where the animator's hand is at a
  moment. It follows the stroke being drawn, lifts and glides between strokes
  of a run, and comes in from and returns to `offstage`. Pure, so scenes scrub.
- **`drawHand` `lift` and `arm` options**: a lifted hand rises off the page
  with a shadow under the tip; `arm` sets how much forearm shows.
- **Characters v2: `character({ hands: 'cartoon' })`**: gloved four-fingered
  hands on the fluid figure, posed by `hand.left.*` / `hand.right.*` pose
  fields (so they are tracks), relative to a hand hanging at the side;
  `characterHandPose()`; `handSize`.
- **Examples:** `examples/headless-video/hand-shapes.mjs` (a model sheet of
  every shape, turned and in every look) and
  `examples/pencil-story-hindi/index.html`, a 30-second Pencilmation-style
  Hindi story (प्यासा राहगीर, the thirsty traveller) made with the browser
  bundle alone: the hand draws the scene and writes the Devanagari narration.
- **Docs:** [Cartoon Hands](../docs/cartoon-hands.md), and the sheet in
  `docs/model-sheet/hand-shapes.png`.

## Changed

- **The drawing hand (`drawHand`) looks natural.** It is the hand rig in its
  pencil grip: the thumb and middle finger pinch the tool, the index finger
  rests on it, the other fingers curl under, and a short forearm's sleeve
  fades out instead of a 2000 px arm crossing the frame. The pencil is 190 px
  at scale 1 (was 240).

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking API changes.** `drawHand` keeps its signature, but the hand
  looks different and is a little smaller; scenes that relied on the old arm
  reaching off screen can pass a longer `arm`.
- Characters keep round hands unless they ask for `hands: 'cartoon'`.
- Not yet: a hand-shape picker in the editor, foreshortened hands when the
  forearm points at the viewer, and cartoon hands on the v1 stick figure.
