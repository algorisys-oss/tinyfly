# v0.103.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-12

Native garba and dandiya choreography: dancers keep their steps while turning,
circle in formations with planted toes, spin through full revolutions, and
bring both dandiya tips to a shared strike point.

## Added

- **Native human dances**: `HUMAN_DANCES`, `humanDanceFrame` and
  `humanDanceTracks` provide editable eight-beat garba (two-clap and
  three-clap) and dandiya routines. Local limb motion, hand shapes, weight
  shifts and recovery stay independent of body heading and roll.
- **Circle formations**: `danceCircleAt` and `danceCircleTracks` coordinate
  equal spacing, ground travel, inward/outward/tangent/fixed facing and
  independent body spins. Support toes stay at their world planting points
  during stance, then release smoothly.
- **World contacts**: `plantDanceFoot` returns an adjusted pose and position
  for a toe or ankle pivot; `meetDandiya` solves two stick tips to one shared
  3D point. Both report unreachable targets.
- **3D script actions**: `spin` accepts signed `turns` and a duration;
  `dance` accepts a native style, tempo and beat count.
- **Editor and example**: native dances appear in Character and 3D Scene
  menus; `examples/headless-video/garba-circle.mjs` is a looping formation.
  The Examples page includes a festive Navratri scene with decorated clothes,
  larger dandiya sticks, partner strikes, circle travel and half/full spins.

## Fixed

- `characterScript3D`'s `face` action honors an explicit duration when
  changing heading. It still takes the shortest turn; use `spin` for full
  revolutions. Explicit durations also apply when already facing the target.
- Scripted dances preserve a preceding hold and blend into the routine
  within their duration. Missing hand fields use `HAND_REST` for anchors and
  entry blending, retaining relaxed hands through holds and explicit zero values;
  hand pose transitions share this fallback. Native editor dances omit hand tracks without a hand rig.
- Circle track bakes reject mismatched member counts and omit constant
  rest-pose fields, keeping exported timelines smaller.
- Sparse custom clips retain the full authored field union with numeric
  base, human/hand rest or zero defaults in frames and both JSON bakers.
- Rolled circle bakes preserve native yaw before roll, grounding and foot
  contacts using pose turn/roll with zero object yaw; zero-roll bakes retain rotateY.
- Unchanged script action-start anchors preserve preceding face/pose and walk-stop easing
  without changing the following action's easing.
- Festive live and headless examples solve contacts with their rendered body
  options, aligning wrists and decorated stick grips.
- Foot IK preserves authored hip turn-out and straight-leg contacts at full reach
  for both ankles and toes.

## Validation

- Tests cover every native routine through front/side/back views, clap
  contact, world toe plants, partner stick contacts, signed spins, audio
  tempo, JSON seeking and circle closure, including late-authored hand fields
  and rendered world joints and planted toes under combined yaw and roll.
- Composition regressions compare standalone and chained actions away from
  the midpoint; both festive examples verify rendered wrists, grips and tips
  through strikes, half turns and full spins.
- The rendered garba-circle example passes the video loop check.
- Hip turn-out regressions cover new ankle targets and unchanged ankle/toe
  contacts on both legs, including all native routines.

## Upgrade Notes

- Existing stick-figure dance APIs and routines remain available.
- Native routines are stylised, editable choreography. A formation that
  travels farther than its legs can reach reports an unplanted stance.
- Contacts alternate left/right each integer beat, starting left, with stance
  through 0.6 of the beat, including custom routines. Different footwork needs
  `plantFeet: false` and explicit contacts. Fractional body spins may need
  multiple orbits to loop; total extra turns must be whole revolutions.
- Native editor dances retain heading/roll; the Character menu keys hands
  only with a hand rig, and the 3D Scene menu omits hand tracks. Partner IK
  and stick drawing remain code APIs, separate from the dance menu.
- Apply **both** outputs of `plantDanceFoot`: `pose` and `position`.
