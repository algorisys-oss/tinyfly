# v0.84.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-05

Characters in 3D scenes. tinyfly's v2 characters now stand in 3D scenes and
are seen by the scene's camera from anywhere: in front, behind, from high
above. They dance and flip as they do in 2D, in their pen looks (clean,
pencil, silhouette) drawn in perspective with faces and cartoon hands, or as
solid figures of shaded capsules, with soft shadows on the floor. This is
milestone 6 of the [3D plan](../docs/3d-support-plan.md), the one films need
first.

## Added

- **The `character` scene object** and **`characterObjects`** (from
  `@algorisys/tinyfly/characters`): `loadScene3D(scene, { kinds: [characterObjects] })`.
  A character has `character()` options, a `height` in metres, a `pose`, a
  `look` (`pen` or `solid`, with `solid` colours and shading) and a `shadow`.
  Its pose fields are tracks on its object (`stage/tum` `arm.right.spread`).
- **Seeing a character through a camera**: `skeletonInView`,
  `stagePlanSpace`, `solvePlanSpace`, `drawCharacterInView`,
  `characterJointsInView`. Seen front-on through a flat camera a character is
  exactly the 2D figure; from the side it is exactly turn 3.
- **Object kinds in scene-3d**: `loadScene3D(scene, { kinds })` adds kinds
  that validate and prepare their objects and, each frame, return meshes
  (shaded, outlined and depth-sorted with the scene's) and drawables (drawn
  whole at their depth). Frames carry their `time`.
- **3D Scene: Dancing Characters** in the gallery's 3D section: two
  characters dance mirror images while the camera orbits and cranes.
- `examples/headless-video/characters-3d-dance.mjs`: a crane shot and a cut,
  rendered to MP4 in Node.

## Changed

- `solveSkeleton` is built on `solvePlanSpace` (forward kinematics in the
  character's own space); it draws exactly as before (golden frames
  unchanged).

## Technical Details

| File | Description |
|------|-------------|
| `src/characters/rig/skeleton.ts` | `solvePlanSpace`, `stagePlanSpace`, `skeletonInView` |
| `src/characters/scene-objects.ts` | The `character` object kind: pen and solid looks, shadows |
| `src/scene-3d/object-kind.ts` | Object kinds: meshes and drawables in the draw order |

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking API changes.**
- Characters in scenes need the characters add-on; `scene-3d` alone stays
  small and never reaches character code.
- Not yet: mouths and brows on the solid look; a pen-drawn character is
  drawn whole, so it is in front of or behind another object, not between.
