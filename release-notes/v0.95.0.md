# v0.95.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-07

Stories get a world. Everyday objects (props) such as cars, buses, bikes,
trees, houses, a helicopter and a plane act from beats the way figures do,
turn toward the camera and draw with the figures' pens. So do four-legged
animals and birds. In 3D scenes, props and characters can be scripted in
world metres, ride one another, sort in depth, are lit by the scene, and come
in a mesh look that is exact with WebGL2.

## Added

### Props

- **Props**: 3D rigs of simple parts (boxes, cylinders, ellipsoids, extruded
  profiles, panels, tubes) on pivots, with named controls and anchors. They
  turn toward the camera (`turn`, `tilt`) and are drawn with the characters'
  pens (`look: 'clean' | 'pencil' | 'silhouette'`) or as line art
  (`style: 'stick'`), with outlines weighted by size and a contact shadow.
- **`propScript(id, prop, beats, options)`**: beats compiled through the
  figures' acting pass, with an `exaggeration` dial. Common actions are
  `hold`, `turn`, `pop` and `vanish`. `checkPropBeats` names the action that
  was probably meant.
- **Vehicles**: `vehicle(spec)`, with the presets `car`, `truck`, `bus`,
  `tractor`, `cart`, `trainCar`, `bike` and `motorbike`. They `drive`,
  `brake`, `bump` and `honk`, and open `door`s and switch on `lights`. Wheels
  of any size roll exactly the distance driven.
- **Environment**: `tree` (sway, shake, shed leaves) and `house` (door,
  lights, chimney smoke, shake).
- **Aircraft**: `helicopter` and `airplane` (take off, fly, hover, loop the
  loop, land), with rotors that blur.
- **Animals**: `quadruped(spec)`, with `horse`, `dog`, `cat` and `cow`.
  Gaits (walk, trot, canter, gallop) keep their footfall patterns in step with
  the distance. They can `sit`, `jump`, use their calls and their own actions
  (`rear`, `buck`, `wag`, `sniff`, `arch`, `pounce`, `graze`), and their tails
  move fluidly.
- **Birds**: `bird(spec)`, with `songbird`, `crow` and `chicken`. Wings fold
  along the body and beat in rhythm, and the head pecks. Birds `hop`, `walk`,
  `fly` and `land` on perches read from anchors; a chicken `flutter`s.
- **Riding and towing in 2D**: `propRide` and `spliceTracks` (a rider drawn
  between the prop's far and near parts, seen through glass and open doors),
  and `propTow` (a cart behind a horse, its wheels rolling the distance).
- `springFollow` (follow-through), effects (dust, exhaust, skids, honk lines,
  leaves, smoke), `propAt`, `describeTarget` and `checkTracks` for props, and
  a props section in the catalog.

### Props and characters in 3D scenes

- **`propObjects`**: the `prop` object kind,
  `{ kind: 'prop', prop: 'car', position, rotation, values }`. A prop is named
  by preset, its controls are tracks on the object, and `propPreset(name)`
  makes one by name.
- **Depth order**: each prop is cut into columns about a metre across, which
  sort at their own depth among other things. Inside a column, faces are drawn
  far to near with their own outlines. Faces inside or against another solid
  part are left out, and a part's `layer` settles parts that sit into each
  other. Geometry nearer than the camera's near plane is clipped.
- **`propScript3D`**: props scripted in world metres. Moves go to `[x, z]` or
  through points on a smooth path, turning first, with wheels, strides,
  wingbeats and rotors keyed to the distance or time (each prop's `moves`).
  Also `face`, `hold`, and the props' own actions.
- **`characterScript3D`**: v2 characters walk, run, sneak or strut to a point
  or along a path with their feet planted (the character object steps from
  `walk`, `walking` and `gait`). Also `pose`, `gag`, `face` and `place`.
- **`propRide3D`**: a rider's hips stay on a seat anchor as the prop moves,
  bobs and pitches, with hops on and off (`RIDING_POSES.astride`,
  `RIDING_POSES.seated`). Characters are drawn part by part at their own
  depths (`characterPartsInView`), so a leg astride a horse shows on each side
  of it.
- **Lit by the scene**: props are lit by the scene's ambient, directional,
  point and spot lights and its fog, with glowing parts on top. A pen
  character's skin takes the light reaching it.
- **The mesh look** (`look: 'mesh'`): props built of the scene's own meshes,
  lit and outlined, with see-through glass, glow as light of its own and a
  disc shadow. Depth order is exact with the WebGL2 renderer.
- Scene materials take `creases: false`, for smooth shapes built of facets.

### Gallery

- **Road Trip**, **Windy Day**, **Helicopter**, **Traffic**,
  **Horse & Cart**, **Farmyard** and **Birds** (2D).
- **3D Scene: Village**: scripted props and a rider with an orbiting camera,
  with day, sunset and night, and pen, stick or mesh looks.

## Changed

- Scene objects that have a `rotateY` field are refused with the fix:
  `rotateY` is a track, so place the object with `rotation: [0, deg, 0]`.
- Object kinds get the scene's `lights` and `fog`. A kind treats them as
  absent when an older scene entry doesn't pass them.

## Testing

- Prop rigs: culling, outlines, wheels rolling the distance, gaits' footfalls,
  sitting with feet planted, birds' wings and pecks.
- 3D: columns and cuts, hidden faces, near-plane clipping, a bus hiding a
  figure behind its front end, lighting by day and night, the mesh look's
  materials.
- World-metre scripts: wheels and strides in step with the distance at every
  moment, paths through points, fliers' heights, and beat checks with
  suggestions.
- Riding: hips on the saddle mid-walk, hops on and off, and part depths.
