# v0.98.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-09

Character appearance, from a 16-sheet everyday-life reference pack. A v2
character (`character()`) now has hair, facial hair, glasses, a hat, ears,
a build, clothes and things in its hands, all as plain data. Hair and hats
are regions on the head rather than flat drawings, so they turn with it
through all eight views. Characters without these options draw exactly as
before.

## Added

- **Hair** (`hair: 'bob'`, or `{ style, color, length, hairline, texture, ties… }`):
  - 34 styles, from `bald` and `buzzCut` to `twinBraids` and `longLocs`.
  - Textures (spiky, curly, wavy, locs), partings and strands, and ponytails,
    buns and braids tied to one place on the head.
  - `HAIR_COLORS`, and `resolveHair` to fill in every field.
  - From the front, long hair hangs behind the shoulders; from behind, the
    hair covers the head and is drawn over the body. A side ponytail stays on
    the character's own side through a turn.
- **Facial hair** (`facialHair`): 20 styles, or `{ moustache, beard, color }`.
  - Moustaches sit on the face, so they move with the mouth.
  - Beards follow the jaw and leave an opening, so every mouth shape and
    lip-sync shape stays readable. Stubble is included.
- **Glasses, hats and ears**:
  - Glasses: `round`, `square`, `sunglasses`. In profile the arm runs back to
    the ear.
  - Hats: `cap`, `beanie`, `hardHat`, `sunHat`, `bowler`, worn over the hair.
  - `ears: true`.
- **Builds** (`build`, and the library's `HUMAN_BUILDS`): `slim`, `tall`,
  `short`, `broad`, `curvy`, `stocky`, `kid`, `child`, `toddler`.
  - New fields `legLength` and `armLength`.
  - `humanGaitStrideLength` takes the leg length, so feet stay planted on
    every build.
- **Clothes as data** (`outfit`): long or no sleeves, shorts, a skirt, a
  collar, a tie, an apron and a lab coat. Each is drawn in the right order
  from the front and from behind.
- **Cast**: `castMember(name, height)` and `CHARACTER_CAST`.
  - Family: boy, girl, young man, young woman, man, woman, grandpa, grandma.
  - Work: doctor, cook, builder, courier, teacher, farmer.
- **Faces**:
  - Marks `blush`, `tears` and `sweat` (pose fields, 0–1).
  - 12 more expressions: laughing, afraid, bored, tired, embarrassed, proud,
    determined, affectionate, hurt, suspicious, relieved, excited.
- **Everyday actions**:
  - Poses: `sleep`, `stretch`, `sitFloor`, `carry`, `lift`, `push`, `pull`,
    `drink`, `phone`, `read`, `type`.
  - `holding: { right: 'mug' }`: mug, phone, book, bag, briefcase, umbrella,
    broom, parcel. Pose fields `held.left`, `held.right` and `held.both` let go.
  - `handGrip(joints, side)` gives the grip point for drawing anything else in
    a hand.
  - `meetHands(kind, a, b)`: a handshake, high five, fist bump or hand-over, as
    two poses that reach one point. `meetingSpacing` says how far apart the two
    should stand, and `reached` says whether both hands got there.
- **Animals**:
  - `lie` for every four-legged animal (`lyingPose`).
  - `glide` for birds: wings held out still, sinking toward `height`.
- **Gallery**: Cast Turnaround, Hair, Beards & Hats (an interactive picker)
  and Hand Over.
- **Editor**:
  - A Head section on the character element: Dress as (a cast member), hair
    and its colour, facial hair, glasses, hat, ears, and items in each hand.
  - Clothing pickers.
  - The new builds, with Legs and Arms sliders.
  - The new poses and expressions in the Body and Face lists.
- **Docs**:
  - A Character Appearance guide, with the appearance model sheet
    (`examples/headless-video/appearance-sheet.mjs`).
  - The catalog lists builds, hairstyles, facial hair, glasses, hats, cast,
    held items and hand meetings.

## Changed

- **Docs viewer**: shows images. Model sheets were bare links before.
- **Layers**: a `CharacterLayer` receives the full pose as a fifth argument.
- **Props docs**: list the new `lie` and `glide` actions.
- **Guides**: the LLM guide and the agent skill describe a character's look
  as data and `meetHands`.
