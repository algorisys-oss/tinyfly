# v0.101.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-11

Loops that close. A video scene can say `loop: true`, and everything needed
to make a loop that repeats exactly follows: motion helpers that are periodic
by construction, a nested zoom that steps one level per loop, faces for
close-ups with a drawing in the pupil, dives with zoom blur and streaking
lines, Penrose figures that line up from one viewpoint, raw meshes and
animatable outlines in 3D with a camera that rolls, goo that merges, drips
and pops, and sound cut to the loop's length.

## Added

- **`loop: true` on a video scene**: the duration is the period. Frames carry
  it as `period`, 3D trails without a `period` of their own wrap at it, and
  `tinyfly video` runs the loop check before it encodes anything (exit 1 when
  the loop does not close).
- **Periodic helpers** that repeat exactly over a period: `wobble` (a sine,
  triangle or square with whole cycles), `loopEase` (an eased there-and-back),
  `loopNoise` (seeded value noise read round a circle), `torusNoise`
  (periodic in time and along a length), `phaseOf` and `pingPong`.
- **Similarity transforms and nested zooms**: `similarityAbout(scale, turn,
  centre)` as a zoom's step, `composeSimilarity`, `powSimilarity` (a fraction
  of a step), `invertSimilarity`, `similarityFixedPoint` and
  `similarityMatrix`; `nestedZoom(ctx, { step, phase, extent, coversAbove,
  draw })` draws only the levels of a Droste picture that show and closes
  after one step; `nestedZoomTransform`. Example
  `examples/headless-video/nested-zoom.mjs`.
- **Faces for close-ups**: pose fields `pupil` (size), `lid` (upper lids
  lowered) and `bulge` (eyes out of the head, whites and all), used by
  `scared`, `afraid`, `sleepy`, `suspicious` and `excited`; `face: { nose:
  true }`; `drawCharacterHead(ctx, who, pose, { x, y, radius })` draws just
  the head big with the line weight scaled; `face.inPupil(ctx, pupil)` draws
  inside each pupil, clipped to it, so a Droste head holds itself.
- **`curve(keys)(x)`**: keyframe curves over any input, with the same easings
  and colour blending as tracks, holding beyond the ends.
- **Dives**: `applyZoomBlur(ctx, { centre, strength, samples, blend })` and
  `drawDiveLines(ctx, { centre, count, phase, seed, … })`, both
  deterministic; the lines loop with a phase.
- **Impossible objects**: the isometric view as geometry (`projectIsometric`,
  `isometricBoxFaces`, `isometricFaces` far to near, `drawIsometricFaces`);
  `penroseTriangle` (three beams whose loop closes one diagonal on, the first
  drawn again over the third's end so every corner looks the same) and
  `penroseStairs` (four flights round a ring, the rise nudged so the last
  step meets the first); `penroseTriangle3D` for 3D scenes with an
  orthographic camera down the diagonal.
- **3D scenes**: a raw `mesh` geometry (`positions`, `indices`, optional
  `normals` and `colors` per face or per vertex); `outlineWidth` and
  `outlineColor` tracks over a material's outline, and outlines that fade
  into the fog; a camera's `up` and `roll` (a `roll` track too; `rollView`);
  `nearFade: { from, to }` so a dive passes through geometry as if it were
  glass.
- **Satisfying matter**: `metaballContours` (blobs that merge through necks
  and split), `dripPath`, `bubblePath` (grows, quivers, repeats over a
  period), `bubblePop`, `squashStretch` keeping the area or the volume, and
  `scalePoints`.
- **Sound for loops**: `loopBeats` (a tempo cut to the period in whole beats),
  `beatPeriod`, `beatPulse` (spikes on every beat and decays, with a bar
  accent), `audioEnvelope` and `envelopeAt` (a level per frame, wrapped for a
  loop); `cutAudioToLoop` (Node) trims a drone or riser to the loop with its
  end blended into its start, on `loopSamples`.
- **Docs**: the Loops chapter of the video guide (a scene that loops, sound,
  nested zooms, dives and impossible objects, satisfying matter), close-ups in
  the Character Appearance guide, impossible objects, raw meshes, outlines,
  roll and the near fade in the 3D guide.

## Changed

- **Outlines fog**: in the Canvas 2D renderer an outline's ink now fades
  toward the fog colour with distance, like the surface it edges; the toon
  golden was regenerated for it.
- **Expressions** carry `pupil`, `lid` and `bulge` (1, 0, 0 unless the face
  uses them), so every expression still sets the same fields.
- **Guides**: the LLM guide and the agent skill tell a model how to make a
  loop that closes.

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking changes.** Scenes without `loop`, fog or the new fields draw
  as before; a 3D scene with fog and outlines now draws its far outlines
  fainter.
- **`HUMAN_REST` and every expression have new fields**: `pupil` (1), `lid`
  (0), `bulge` (0).
- **`DrawTriangle.ink`** carries the resolved outline; renderers that read
  `material.outline` directly should read `ink` instead.
