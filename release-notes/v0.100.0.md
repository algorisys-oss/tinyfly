# v0.100.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-11

The world around the characters, and the editor's stage in every renderer.
Elements are selected, dragged and resized on the stage in the Canvas and SVG
previews as they always were in the DOM one; v2 characters reach with their
own hands and feet when they act a beat script; two people hug and help each
other up; the wardrobe gains hoodies, cardigans, overalls, saris and boots;
props gain furniture with the places a figure uses them at, a wheelchair, a
sky and a room (`scenery`), seven more animals and birds that grip a perch;
faces open on teeth and a tongue, feet come in shapes, and a cane goes in any
hand. A wheelchair and a cane are accessories, not an age.

## Added

- **On-stage editing in every renderer**: the Canvas and SVG previews get a
  transparent hit layer with a box per element (and group child) carrying the
  DOM preview's pointer handlers, registered with the DOM adapter so the
  boxes follow the animation and the camera. The selection chrome and the
  stage overlays (guides, rulers, camera pan, the pen) are shared by all
  three renderers, and Grid, Snap, Rulers, Pen and Pan work in each.
- **Beat scripts in the character's proportions**: `ScriptOptions.reach`
  (`{ arm, leg }`) scales the stick script's measured reach, and
  `characterScript` sets it from the v2 body (`reachOf(who)`), so the figure
  steps to where its own hand or foot lands; while a beat's hand or foot is on
  its target the limb is re-aimed with `reachCharacter` (a point at the spot,
  a grab or put on it, a kick a foot short for the toe, a swipe, write or
  push following the stick palm, the body leaning down to a low one).
- **Hair follows the head's place in the pose**: `hairFollowTracks` reads
  where the head is in the pose every 16 ms (a gait's bob, a crouch and a
  jump move it), with the springs' four values sampled from their own tracks.
- **Two people, more than hands**: `embrace(a, b)` (a hug: arms over the
  other's shoulder and round the waist, leaning in, heads together, from
  where they stand; `embraceSpacing`) and `helpUp(helper, sitter, progress)`
  (from the floor to standing with the hands joined, the helper bowing and
  straightening; `helpUpSpacing`); everyday cycles `humanCyclePose('sweeping'
  | 'brushing', phase)` run like a gait.
- **Wardrobe as data**: `outfit.over` adds a `hoodie` (a hood behind the neck
  from the front, down the back from behind, a front pocket, drawstrings), a
  `cardigan` (open down the front over the shirt, buttoned), `overalls` (a
  bib in the trousers' colour with straps over the shoulders, crossing the
  back) and a `sari` (a pleated drape to the ankles, the pallu over the near
  shoulder); `outfit.shoes: 'boots'` with `shoeColor`. Editor **Over** and
  **Shoes** pickers.
- **Furniture with the places a figure uses them at**: `chair()` (`seat`,
  `back`, `front`; tips back on its rear legs and settles, falls over),
  `desk()` (`top`, `edge`, `drawer`, a `chair` spot; the drawer slides out),
  `bed()` (`mattress`, `pillow`, `foot`, `side`; the mattress gives and
  springs back) and `door()` in its frame (`handle`, `doorway`, `front`,
  `beyond`; opens on its hinge, slams, rattles when knocked; the doorway
  shows when open), in the preset catalog and the 3D palette.
- **A wheelchair**: `wheelchair()`, a seat and back on a frame with two big
  rimmed wheels, castors, footrests and push handles; `roll`s to a place with
  its wheels turning exactly the distance (and a `roll` move in metres for
  3D), `brake`s with them locked, tips back for a `wheelie`; its `seat` is
  ridden in `RIDING_POSES.wheeling` and a pusher stands at `handles`.
- **A cane**: `holding: { right: 'cane' }`, a crook in the hand and the shaft
  to the ground while the hand hangs, lifting with it once it rises;
  `HUMAN_POSES.cane` stands leaning on it.
- **Scenery**: `scenery()` draws a sky (the sun and moon on their arcs, stars,
  clouds drifting on the wind, rain that slants with it, snow, hills or a lit
  city on the horizon, the ground) or a room with a window on it. `hour`,
  `clouds`, `wind`, `rain` and `snow` are tracks and every frame is drawn
  from its time; the wind streams hair (`hairFollowTracks`' `wind`).
  `scenePreset` and `buildScene` give park, street, office and home scenes.
- **Animals**: `lie` for every four-legged animal (`lyingPose`) and `glide`
  for birds; perch contact (a bird's `grip` curls its toes round a perch as
  it lands above the ground, cleared as it takes off); seven more species:
  goat (horns, a beard; bleats, grazes), rabbit (tall ears; hops, squeaks),
  squirrel (a bushy tail; scampers, chatters), pigeon (coos), duck (a broad
  bill; quacks), owl (a big round head, slow wingbeats; hoots) and parrot (a
  hooked beak, a long tail; squawks).
- **Faces and feet**: `face: { teeth, tongue }` shows a row of upper teeth
  hanging from the top of the mouth and a tongue lying in the bottom of it
  once the mouth opens past 0.3, built from the opening's real edges so they
  stay inside a grin, a wail or an O; `feet` is `round`, `flat`, `pointed`
  or `big`. Editor **Teeth**, **Tongue** and **Feet** controls.
- **Editor**: **📊 Chart** element (kind, data and series as JSON, title,
  theme, max, value labels, axis titles; bad data named) drawn in both
  previews and the exporters, which a Character's **Beats** aim at, change
  and ride; the Beats compiler rides every surface.
- **Gallery**: Help Up, Park Day.
- **Docs**: hugs, help-ups and cycles, clothes, teeth and feet, the cane in
  the Character Appearance guide; furniture, the wheelchair, the animals and
  Scenery in the props guide; on-stage editing in every renderer, the Chart
  element and the new pickers in the editor guide.

## Changed

- **`hairFollowTracks` is five times faster**: the springs sample a timeline
  of only the four tracks they read, and the whole pose only every 16 ms.
- **Guides**: the LLM guide, the agent skill and the capability catalog list
  the new props, species, clothes, face options, the wheelchair and the cane.

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking changes.** Plain faces, round feet and the existing outfits,
  props and poses draw as before.
- **New names in the catalogs**: `HUMAN_POSES.cane`, `RIDING_POSES.wheeling`,
  `HELD_ITEMS.cane`, `FOOT_SHAPES`, and the `chair`, `desk`, `bed`, `door`,
  `wheelchair`, `goat`, `rabbit`, `squirrel`, `pigeon`, `duck`, `owl` and
  `parrot` presets. Code that enumerates `HELD_ITEMS` or `PROP_PRESETS` sees
  them.
- **`characterScript` steps differently**: the figure now walks to where its
  own hand or foot reaches, so a scripted character's stopping place can move
  a little from v0.99.0.
