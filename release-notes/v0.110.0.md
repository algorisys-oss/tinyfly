# v0.110.0 — Cricket review zoom and reliable character turns

Released: 2026-10-13

The organic cricket cast follows the play through run-up, delivery and stroke.
The interactive example keeps its controls within reach alongside the scene.

## Added

- Public camera `zoom` option (0.25–4, default 1) for every cricket view,
  including orbit and ball follow. The example adds zoom-in/out, a percentage
  slider and reset; selected zoom is included in downloaded story JSON.

- `cast: 'men'` and public `CRICKET_CASTS` labels. All players and umpires use
  the existing youngMan organic preset; other cast choices remain available.

- `CRICKET_FIELDING_POSITIONS` exports eleven reusable stock labels, also stored
  in player JSON names. The collecting actor is Extra cover. An accessible
  Teams and field positions guide explains the batting XI's nine off-field
  teammates, off/on sides and mid-on versus its deeper long-on alternative.

## Fixed

- Rear scalp hair and hats render with the head in globally sorted 3D scenes.
  Turning or nodding characters no longer acquire bald patches when the skin
  pass sorts after the final body part. Existing 2D rendering is retained.

- Every stock fielder watches the bowler, smoothly picks up the
  incoming delivery at release, tracks its flight/bounce and follows the ball
  after contact or a miss. Close fielders no longer turn to the batter early. Head aiming
  uses the native neck frame and world targets, independently of the camera.
- Right-handed batting uses a side-on guard, supported feet and a grounded
  ready bat near the back foot. The left hand sits above the right on the
  handle; backlift, blade contact and follow-through reuse actual rig palms.
- Bowler watches the batter during the run-up. Runners face their travel;
  collectors turn toward the batter and throw toward the receiving wicket.
- Example selection, camera, playback, speed and scrub controls move into a
  panel beside the scene, or above it on narrow screens, with visible focus.

- Keeper-bound misses pass outside the positive-x off stump and clear the
  striker's native legs throughout the delivery, preserving keeper palms,
  bowled/LBW contact and the later stumping wicket break.
