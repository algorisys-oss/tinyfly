# v0.102.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-11

A film from a 3D scene, as data. Shots cut and blend between cameras, camera
moves are tracks, sets get skies, shadows and a mood, characters speak and
look at each other, the sound is mixed and ducked, and the frames can be
motion-blurred, supersampled and written as ProRes. Underneath, the Canvas
2D path gains a software depth buffer, so crossing shapes and see-through
meshes draw right in Node too.

## Added

- **A software depth buffer** (`drawScene3D(…, { depthBuffer: true })`,
  `SoftwareRenderer`, `rasterizeResolvedScene`): every triangle rasterised
  with a depth per pixel; see-through meshes blended after the opaque ones;
  ink depth-tested so an outline never shows through what is in front;
  layers keep their meaning; objects drawn whole go on top. A golden pins it.
- **Shots and cameras** (`scene-3d/shots.ts`): `Shot { id, start, end,
  camera, label, transition }`, `shotTracks` (the `activeCamera` cut track),
  `shotMarkers`, `shotAt`, `checkShots`, `snapShotsToBeats`; transitions
  (`cut`, `crossfade`, `dipToBlack`, `wipe`) any frame can ask about with
  `transitionAt`, drawn by `drawTransition` with `withActiveCamera`; camera
  moves as tracks (`cameraMoveTracks`: orbit, dolly, crane, pan, tilt, zoom,
  any mix; `cameraFollowTracks`; `cameraHandheldTracks`, a seeded shake);
  aspects and safe areas (`FRAME_ASPECTS`, `frameRect`, `frameSize`,
  `safeAreas`, `drawLetterbox`, `drawSafeAreas`). Example `film-shots.mjs`.
- **Sets and mood**: a sky as a gradient `background: { top, bottom }`, the
  scene's `ground`, blob shadows under meshes (`shadow: true`), `ground()`
  for a floor, and lighting presets (`lightingPreset('day' | 'dusk' | 'night'
  | 'stage')`) sharing light ids so `lightingTracks(from, to)` is a change of
  mood as tracks. A dusk golden.
- **Characters acting in 3D**: `say` (lip-synced, a talking head,
  `speechLength`) and `look` (a point, a height or `'camera'`; `lookFields`)
  beats in `characterScript3D`; `characterLookAtTracks` keeps a character
  looking at another, a point or the camera as both move
  (`objectPositionAt`); `stageSpots` places several figures in a line, an
  arc, a circle or a seeded cluster.
- **Sound for a film**: `mixAudio` (a looped bed, narration, effects at
  times, gains, fades, ducking with attack and release, soft-limited) and
  `renderMix` in Node, which decodes the files and writes the WAV for the
  scene's `audio`.
- **Rendering**: `motionBlur: { samples, shutter }` (deterministic sub-frame
  averaging), `supersample: 2` (anti-aliasing), and ProRes 422 HQ output
  (`codec: 'prores'`, `--codec prores`, a `.mov` with PCM audio).
- **Docs**: Shots and cameras, Sets and light for mood, speaking and looking,
  the depth buffer in the 3D guide; a sound mix, motion blur, supersampling
  and ProRes in the video guide; the LLM guide and the skill tell a model how
  a film is made from data.

## Changed

- **The editor's 3D background picker** shows a sky gradient's bottom colour.
- **Resolved triangles** carry `depths` per corner and `outlineDepths` per
  ink edge, for renderers with a depth buffer.

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking changes.** Scenes without the new fields draw as before.
- **`DrawTriangle`** has new required fields (`depths`, `outlineDepths`);
  code that builds triangles by hand must fill them.
- **`ResolvedScene3D.background`** may now be a `{ top, bottom }` gradient;
  custom renderers reading it as a string should handle both.
