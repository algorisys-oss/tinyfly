# v0.77.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-03

Characters v2, milestone 1. A new character system sits beside the stick
figure: one skeleton with its joints in 3D, drawn as flat lines. A figure turns
from the front to the side to the back, its far arm goes behind its body and its
face slides round and is gone from behind, all without special views. Whatever
touches the ground rests on it, so sitting, kneeling, crawling and lying down are
just poses. It draws in a clean, pencil (Pencilmation) or silhouette look, as a
fluid or a traditional stick figure, and the editor has a Character element you
pose and keyframe with a few clicks. The v1 stick figure is unchanged, apart from
two fixes for turned figures.

![Human turnaround sheet](../docs/model-sheet/human-turnaround.png)

## Added

- **`character()`, `drawCharacter()`, `characterJoints()`**: a character from
  options (figure `fluid` or `stick`, look `clean`, `pencil` or `silhouette`,
  height, `proportions: 'bold' | 'thin'`, colours, layers), drawn in a pose with
  its feet at (0, 0), and where every part is, as drawn.
- **Turning:** the `turn` pose field: 0 front, 1 side (facing right), 2 back,
  3 the other side, anything in between. Sideways spread shows from the front,
  forward swing from the side; parts are drawn far to near.
- **Contact:** the lowest of the feet, knees, hands, hips, back or head rests
  on the ground; `lift` raises the figure, `roll` rolls it in the picture plane.
- **Reaching:** `reachCharacter()` solves an arm or leg so its end lands on a
  point, in any view.
- **The human:** `humanPlan()`, `humanPose()`, `HUMAN_POSES` (standing, wave,
  point, cheer, hands on hips, think, shrug, sit, kneel, crouch, crawl, lie
  down), `HUMAN_EXPRESSIONS`, `humanFieldLabel()` (plain-language field names).
- **Looks:** a shared `Pen` (`createPen()`): clean tapered strokes; pencil
  strokes that boil, with pressure along each stroke, faint construction lines
  and the odd rubbed-out earlier attempt; silhouettes for readability checks.
  Layer hooks draw through the same pen, so clothes match the look.
- **Clothes:** `basicOutfit({ shirt, trousers })`, a T-shirt and trousers whose
  sleeves grow out of the shoulders.
- **Timeline:** `characterTarget()` (every pose field is a prop, so tracks
  animate it), `characterAt()` for immediate-mode drawing that follows it,
  `characterPoseTracks()` (timed poses to tracks), `mixPoses()`.
- **Editor:** a 🧍 **Character** element. Pick its figure, look, colours,
  clothes, view, body pose and face, then **◆ Keyframe pose at playhead**; move
  the playhead, pick the next pose, keyframe again. Pose tracks read in plain
  words ("Right arm · out / in"). Shows in the DOM and Canvas previews and in GIF
  / WebP / MP4 export.
- **Model sheets** in `docs/model-sheet/`, drawn by code: the v1 model sheet
  (two dressed characters in five views) and the v2 human turnaround.
- **Plans:** `docs/character-system-plan.md` (people, hair, animals, birds from
  six reference sheets) and `docs/character-system-m1.md`.

## Changed

- **v1 stick figure, turned figures:** from the front the feet turn out to
  their own sides, swinging round with `turn`; past `turn` 0.25 the back arm
  and its sleeve draw behind the torso and `body` layer.
- `taperedLine()` and `rubberLimb()` moved to `characters/look/` (still
  exported, unchanged); `taperedOutline()` added.

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking API changes.** The v2 character system is new API beside the v1
  stick figure, which keeps its API and its classic look (golden frames pass).
- v1 figures with `turn` above 0.25 now hide their back arm behind the body, and
  front-facing feet turn out: turned figures look slightly different.
- Not yet: characters in exported HTML / embeds and in the SVG preview.
