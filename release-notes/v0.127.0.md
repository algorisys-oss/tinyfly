# v0.127.0 — Cosmic explainer scenes and sound effects from numbers

Released: 2026-11-17

Animated science explainers have a recognisable look: bright flat colour, a
gradient sky per scene, drifting dust and soft bokeh, shapes shaded without
outlines, a dive through scale from a hand down to one atom, captions two
words at a time, and a whoosh or a pop on every move. tinyfly could draw all
of it by hand; now it is a kit, and the sound comes from numbers.

## Added

- **Cosmic explainer kit** (`@algorisys/tinyfly/characters`, docs/cosmic-explainer.md):
  - skies and depth: `drawSkyGradient` (`COSMIC_SKIES`: space, micro,
    magenta, cyan, lime, sunset, ocean), `drawDust` (three parallax depths,
    twinkling, moved by a camera `offset`), `drawBokeh`, `dustTwinkle`;
  - flat-shaded shapes: `drawShadedBall` (a shadow crescent and a
    highlight), `drawIsoCube` / `isoCubeFaces`, `drawHaze` (a probability
    cloud on a ring), `cosmicShade`, `parseRgb`;
  - zooming through scale: `drawZoomLevels` draws levels `factor` apart in
    growing portals and skips what a covering portal hides; `depthAt`,
    `zoomAt` (log space), `zoomLevelLayout`, `drawPortalRim`, `levelPresence`;
  - short captions and chips: `drawShortCaption` (one to three words at a
    time, in step with the line), `captionChunkAt`, `captionChunks`
    (Devanagari works), `drawLabelChip`, `drawCornerNote`.
- **Sound effects from numbers** (`@algorisys/tinyfly/headless`):
  `soundWhoosh`, `soundPop`, `soundThud`, `soundShimmer`, `soundTick`,
  `soundClick` and `soundPad` return seeded mono `Float32Array` samples; no
  files to find or license.
- **`COSMIC_KIT`** in the capabilities catalog (`npx tinyfly capabilities`).
- **`examples/headless-video/cosmic-explainer.mjs`**: a 9:16 science short
  with a dive through scale, captions, chips and a generated sound mix.

## Changed

- **`renderMix` layers take `samples`** as well as `file`: generated sounds
  join a mix without ffmpeg decoding them.

## Docs

- docs/cosmic-explainer.md; README; the examples table; `docs/llm-guide.md`
  and the tinyfly skill list the kit and the sound generators.

## Validation

- Unit tests: sky colours, dust determinism and motion, bokeh, the ball's
  shadow and highlight, cube shading, the haze on its ring, log-space zoom,
  key checks, the level layout and portals by pixel, caption chunks
  (Devanagari included) and timing, chip pops, the catalog; sounds audible,
  unclipped, the asked length, deterministic per seed, a whoosh sweeping up
  or down, sounds in `mixAudio` at their times, `renderMix` with samples and
  no ffmpeg.
- The cosmic-explainer example rendered to MP4 and checked frame by frame.
