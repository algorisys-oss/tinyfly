# v0.78.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-03

Animated maps. A new `@algorisys/tinyfly/maps` module draws maps that animate:
the camera flies from the whole world in to a city, pins drop in, and routes
draw themselves while a marker travels them. It works on OpenStreetMap tiles in
the browser, or on a bundled offline world outline (also in pencil) that
renders the same everywhere, including headless video. The editor gains a 🗺
Map element: add places from a city list and animate the whole trip with one
click.

## Added

- **`@algorisys/tinyfly/maps`:**
  - Web Mercator: `project()`, `unproject()`, `fitView()` (show every place),
    `flyView()` (a flight that pulls out between far places), `greatCircle()`.
  - **Offline world outline** from Natural Earth 1:110m (public domain):
    `drawWorld()` with highlighted countries and pencil borders,
    `worldCountries()`, `findCountry()`.
  - **Raster tiles**: OpenStreetMap by default (`OSM_TILES`) or any
    `{z}/{x}/{y}` server; `TileCache` (each tile fetched once, parent tiles
    stand in while loading), `preloadTiles()` for complete frames, the tile
    server's credit drawn on the map.
  - **`mapTarget()`**: a canvas target with places (pins that drop in) and
    routes (flight arc, great circle or straight; draw on; a travelling arrow
    or dot). Its view (`view.lon`, `view.lat`, `view.zoom`), pins
    (`<place>.show`) and routes (`<route>.draw`, `<route>.marker`) are props, so
    timeline tracks animate them.
  - `mapFlyTracks()` (a camera flight as keyframes), `routeStops()` (when the
    line reaches each stop), `mapAt()` (scene positions in a frame),
    `WORLD_CITIES` and `findCity()` (about 80 major cities).
- **CDN add-on `tinyfly-maps.iife.js`**: load it after `tinyfly.iife.js` and it
  adds the maps functions to the same `tinyfly` global. It is separate so the
  main bundle does not grow by the world map's 33 KB (gzipped).
- **Editor: 🗺 Map element.** A street map, the world outline or a pencil
  outline; places from a city picker or by latitude and longitude; a route with
  its shape, colour, dashes and marker; fit to places, whole world and zoom;
  **◆ Keyframe view at playhead** (once animated, view changes are keyed at the
  playhead); **✨ Animate trip from playhead**, which builds the pins, route and
  marker tracks in one undo step. Map tracks read in plain words ("View · zoom",
  "Delhi · pin"). Maps show in both previews and in GIF / WebP / MP4 export,
  which waits for each frame's tiles.
- **Examples:** a **Map Route** gallery card (OpenStreetMap tiles, the offline
  outline, or pencil) and `examples/headless-video/map-route.mjs` (an offline
  trip, rendered without a browser or a network).
- **Docs:** [Animated Maps](../docs/maps.md), and a Maps section in the editor
  guide.

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking changes.** Maps are a new entry point and a new editor element.
- **OpenStreetMap's tile servers are for light use**, with attribution (drawn for
  you). For busy pages or bulk rendering, point `url` at your own tile server or
  a commercial provider; the offline outline needs no network.
- Not yet: maps in exported HTML / embeds and the SVG preview, and a tile
  loader for headless video (it uses the offline outline).
