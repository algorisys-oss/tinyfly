# v0.36.0

**Release Date:** 2026-07-22

A **Camera inspector** — pan, zoom, and rotate the whole stage from the Property
Panel, keyframed at the playhead. No more hand-editing raw Camera tracks.

## Highlights

- **🎥 Camera section in the Property Panel.** When a camera exists and nothing
  is selected, the Property Panel shows **Pan X / Pan Y / Zoom / Rotation** inputs.
- **Keyframes at the playhead.** Scrub to a time, dial in the framing, and the
  pan/zoom/rotate is recorded — each field upserts a keyframe at the current time
  (updating the one already there instead of piling up duplicates).
- **Reset & remove.** "Reset to identity" snaps the camera back at the playhead;
  "Remove camera" drops all Camera tracks.

## How it works

Two new store methods drive it: `getCameraValue(prop)` reads the live pan/zoom/
rotate at the playhead, and `setCameraValue(prop, value)` upserts a keyframe on
the matching `Camera` track (creating the track if the property has none). The
inspector is just a thin UI over those, so the same behaviour is scriptable via
the API.

## Added

- `getCameraValue` / `setCameraValue` on the editor store (+ tests).
- Camera section in `property-panel.tsx` (+ `.property-hint` / `.secondary-button`
  styles).
