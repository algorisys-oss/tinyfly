# v0.99.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-11

Characters in motion and the story around them. Hanging hair swings after a
turn and streams back on a run; v2 characters act the same beat scripts the
stick figure does, and the AI prompt bar writes those scripts; charts get pies,
donuts, several series and axis titles; props have floors to ride and parts
that come loose; and the editor gains a Code element, a Beats box on the
Character element and props in the 3D palette.

## Added

- **Hair in motion** (`hair.swing`, `hair.trail`, `hair.lift` pose fields):
  - Long hair, ponytails and braids lag behind the head: round it after a turn,
    back on a run, down on a jump and up on the landing. The roots, the cap,
    buns, beards and hats stay put.
  - `hairFollow(sample, options)` keys the lag ahead of time on springs, so
    the same motion always gives the same keys and drawing stays stateless;
    `hairMotionAt(keys, t)` reads it per frame; `hairFollowTracks(target,
    tracks, who)` adds the tracks from a character target's own `x`, `y`,
    `turn` and `head.turn`.
  - Four-legged animals: an `ears` control that lays the ears back on a run,
    on a spring beside the tail.
- **Beat scripts for v2 characters**: `characterScript(target, who, beats,
  options)` compiles the stick-figure script's beats (gaits, `say`, `look`,
  poses, gags, `zip`, `grab`, `throw`…) for a `character()`: key poses onto
  the human body (mirrored when facing left, so `turn` runs negative), the
  human acting pass and lip-sync, and the walk phase keyed to the character's
  own stride (`characterStrideLength`). `characterTarget` steps its gait from
  `walk`, `walking` and `gait` tracks (`characterPoseOf`), with a `gaits`
  option for your own.
- **The AI prompt bar writes beat scripts**: a brief with a story in it gets a
  Character from the cast (`cast`), with `beats` rather than keyframes,
  compiled on load and kept on the element. Beats that would not play are
  named by element and beat before anything loads.
- **Charts**:
  - `kind: 'pie' | 'donut'`: slices share the circle by value; `show` opens a
    hidden slice, `set` re-shares, `pull` stands a slice out, and
    `piece('slice:ID')` makes one loose for `fling`, `follow` and `move`.
  - Several series (`series`, `values` per datum): grouped bars or one line
    per series, a legend (`legend:SERIES`), places and edits as
    `bar:ID:SERIES`, `ride` floors for every bar.
  - Axis titles (`axis: { x, y }`) and theme palettes.
- **Props as floors and loose parts**: `propSurface().piece('part:ID')` makes
  a part (with the parts built on it) a piece for `fling`, `follow` and `move`,
  also as `fling` and `move` edits from a beat's `then`; `ride()` makes every
  anchor and part a floor that carries a figure as the prop lifts, drives or is
  squashed. Whole-prop controls (`lift`, `turn`) resolve to the box.
- **Riders**: `RIDING_POSES.driving` and `.cycling`, `pedallingPose(phase)`
  and `pedallingKeys({ start, end, cadence })`.
- **Boards**: the `lines` theme (ruled paper with a margin); arrows bend round
  texts they would cross, and `bend` sets the bow; a handwriting font, Patrick
  Hand, when the page loads it (the editor and examples do).
- **Editor**:
  - **⌨ Code** element: a code panel in a box that grows to its lines, with
    language, font size, line numbers, hidden lines and the listing in the
    property panel; its line props are tracks.
  - **Beats** on the Character element: beats as JSON, compiled in the chosen
    style and kept on the element; places on Code elements (`{ surface,
    anchor }`) and `then` cues that become tracks on them; problems named by
    beat.
  - **Prop** in the 3D Scene palette, with a preset picker, look and style.
  - Character previews step the gait from walk tracks.
- **Gallery**: Character Acting, Pies & Series, Kick the Tyre; the Cast
  Turnaround card's hair swings.
- **Docs**: Hair in motion in the Character Appearance guide; v2 beat scripts,
  pies and series, loose parts and floors, boards in the acting guide; the
  Code element, Beats and props in the editor guide; rider poses in the 3D
  guide.

## Upgrade Notes

- **No breaking changes.** Single-series charts, plain characters and props
  draw as before.
- **`HUMAN_REST` has new fields**: `hair.swing`, `hair.trail`, `hair.lift` (0).
- **`propSurface().piece` and `ride` now do something**: a prop's parts come
  loose and its anchors and parts are floors; a control of the whole prop
  (`control:lift`) resolves to the prop's box instead of throwing.
- **Chart themes** carry a `palette`; a custom theme object without one keeps
  the light palette.
