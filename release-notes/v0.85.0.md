# v0.85.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-05

3D scenes in the editor. A new **🧊 3D Scene** element holds a real 3D scene
on the stage, seen through its camera, and the property panel builds it
without code: add shapes, characters and lights, move and key them, dance
characters from the playhead, choose camera views and cut between cameras.
It draws in the previews and in every export. This is milestone 4 of the
[3D plan](../docs/3d-support-plan.md), first version.

## Added

- **3D Scene element** (`scene3d`): it starts with a camera at three-quarters,
  a key light, a floor and a box. Its scene is `@algorisys/tinyfly/scene-3d`
  JSON; its tracks are on the element, named `object.property`
  (`box.rotateY`, `character.arm.right.spread`), and `activeCamera` cuts
  between cameras.
- **Property panel**:
  - objects: pick, add (box, sphere, cylinder, cone, ring, star, character,
    light; each on the next free spot) and remove;
  - transform (X, Y, Z, Tilt, Turn, Roll, Scale), keyed at the playhead once
    animated, or all at once;
  - meshes: colour, shading (toon, smooth, flat, unlit), ink outline; lights:
    colour and intensity;
  - characters: look (clean, pencil, silhouette, solid) and **Dance from
    playhead**;
  - cameras: field of view, look through, **Cut to this camera at playhead**;
  - camera view presets (front, ¾, side, top), background and fog.
- `CustomTarget.acceptsProp`: a custom canvas target can take animated
  properties it cannot list up front.

## Changed

- The editor's `keyValuesAtPlayhead` keys any value, not only numbers (a cut
  keys a camera id).
