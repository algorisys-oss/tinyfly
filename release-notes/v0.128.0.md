# v0.128.0 — Comedy cartoon look: bigger eyes, a tie, inked sets

Released: 2026-11-18

Three small options for the big-head comedy cartoon style: googly eyes that
fill the face, a necktie on the bean figure, and furniture outlined as
heavily as the characters standing in front of it.

## Added

- **Bigger, rounder oval eyes**: `face: { eyeScale, eyeAspect }` now sizes
  `style: 'oval'` eyes (the bean figure's) as well as anime eyes. Scale
  0.5–1.2 makes them bigger and moves them apart so they still nearly touch;
  aspect 0.45–1.2 below 1 makes them rounder
  (`face: { style: 'oval', eyeScale: 1.2, eyeAspect: 0.8 }`). Glasses and
  aviator shades fit the resized eyes. `ovalEye(face)` returns their centre
  and radii.
- **A tie on the bean figure**: `bean: { tie: '#1f2937' }` draws a knot at
  the collar and a pointed blade down the chest. It slides toward the side
  the body turns to, narrows with the turn, hides in profile and from
  behind, and sits under a trench coat. An `outfit`'s `tie` maps onto a bean.
- **Inked sets**: `buildScene(name, { ink, lineWidth })` outlines every
  prop in the preset in that colour and width, so a set matches heavily
  inked characters.
