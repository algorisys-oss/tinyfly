# v0.45.2

**Release Date:** 2026-07-22

Pen-tool polish: adjust points and curves as you draw.

## Highlights

- **Drag an anchor** you've already placed to move it — its handles travel with
  it.
- **Drag a handle** to reshape a curve; the opposite handle mirrors it so the
  anchor stays smooth.
- Handles take priority over anchors when they overlap, so a handle sitting on its
  point is always grabbable.

No more finishing and re-editing to fix a point mid-draw.

## How it works

The pen drag became a small discriminated union — `new` (placing a point and
pulling its handles), `handle` (reshaping a curve, with mirrored opposite), and
`anchor` (moving a point and its handles). Mouse-down hit-tests the existing nodes
(handles first, then the close target, then anchors) before falling back to
placing a new point.
