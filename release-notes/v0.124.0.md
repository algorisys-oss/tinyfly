# v0.124.0 — Natural gaits and reaches

Released: 2026-11-16

A review of how the stick figure and the human character move found three
things that read as unnatural in motion. They are fixed in both rigs, and
gaits gain two fields to describe them.

## Added

- Gait fields (`GAITS`, `defineGait`, `gaitPose`, `humanGaitPose`):
  - `flight: true`: the bounce peaks with the legs furthest apart and the
    figure is on the ground as they pass (a run), instead of peaking as they
    pass (a walk).
  - `give` (degrees): the knee of the leg carrying the weight bends under it,
    its thigh forward by half as much so the foot stays under the hips, and
    both knees stay a little soft.
- `gaitBob(spec, angle)` and `gaitGive(spec, lift)` in
  `@algorisys/tinyfly/characters`, shared by both rigs.

## Changed

- **Run**: in the air at the split and landing on a bent knee as the legs
  pass (it was the other way round, on stiff legs). `run` gets `flight` and
  `give: 30`; `bouncy` and `doubleBounce` get a little give.
- **Swinging arms hang plumb**: the lean and the curve of the back no longer
  tip walking arms back with the chest (`tired` trailed its arms behind it).
  `walkPose`, `gaitPose` and `humanGaitPose`. A gait's `shoulder` (paws held
  up) is now measured from the vertical; `sneak`'s is 25 (was 55), which
  looks the same.

## Fixed

- `reachPose` / `reachCharacter`: a limb reaching forward and up came back
  as `spread: 180` (out to the side and over), so blending to it swept the
  arm round sideways. Of the two equivalent solutions it now takes the one
  nearer the limb's current angles.

## Validation

- Unit tests: the run's lift at the split and on the ground as the legs pass,
  its bent landing knee (both rigs); a tired walker's hand under its shoulder;
  a forward-up reach that swings rather than spreads and still lands exactly.
- Contact sheets of every gait at eight phases, and every pose at front, ¾
  and profile, in both rigs, were rendered and reviewed before and after.
