# v0.125.0 — No gimbal lock in the human rig

Released: 2026-11-16

The 3D human rig applied a limb's spread after its swing, about the body's
front-to-back axis. A limb swung straight forward points along that axis, so
spread, and a forearm's bend, did nothing there: a seated figure could not
open its knees and arms held forward could not bring the hands together.
Bones now turn the way joints do, and both angles work at any angle.

## Changed

- **Bones turn in their parent bone's frame**: an elbow bends about the upper
  arm and a knee about the thigh, wherever they point. A bone's angles are
  no longer added to its parent's.
- **Swing and spread are one tilt**: the end of a hanging bone moves forward
  by the swing and out by the spread, like a leaning joystick, however large
  either is. Swing alone and spread alone turn exactly as before. Only a bone
  tilted straight up from hanging is past spreading. A large swing and spread
  together tilt a little further than either: a thigh swung level with its
  knee spread 30° rises about 5°.
- Ready-made poses: `lift`, `read` and `type` now bring the hands together in
  front, as their forearm `bend` always asked; `sitFloor`'s knees open wider.
  Every other pose moves by 3% of the height or less.
- `PlanSpace.chains[id].frames` (from `solvePlanSpace`) holds each bone's
  axes (its left, up and forward in character space) instead of summed
  `{ swing, spread }` angles.

## Fixed

- `reachPose` / `reachCharacter` solve in the new frames. A knee stays a
  hinge: the leg turns about the line from hip to target until the shin
  needs no sideways tilt, so leg reaches stay exact.

## Validation

- Unit tests: swing-only and spread-only turns unchanged; the tilt and its
  inverse round-trip; seated knees spread; forward arms swing in and their
  forearms bend in; an elbow bent with the arm out sideways brings the
  forearm forward, level; exact leg reaches with hip turn-out.
- Every ready-made pose compared joint by joint against the old rig, and
  rendered at front, ¾ and profile before and after.
