# v0.97.0

**Release Date:** 2026-10-08

Props become surfaces. Anything in the scene with controls (a car, a house,
a tree) can be changed by a figure the way a code panel or a chart can: a
figure grabs the door and it swings open, points at a window and the lights
come on. On npm this release also brings everything from v0.96.0 (surfaces,
`surfaceScript`, whiteboards and charts), which was tagged but not published
there.

## Added

- **`propSurface(target, { tracks? })`**: any prop target as a surface.
  - Places: `anchor:NAME` (the rig's anchors, such as `door`, `doorstep`,
    `seat`, `branch`), `part:ID` (one part as seen, such as `window-1` or
    `wheel-0-left`), `control:NAME` (where the parts a control moves or lights
    are) and `box`.
  - Places are worked out from the prop's values at a time, so they follow a
    door as it swings. Pass the prop's own script (`tracks`) and they follow
    it as it drives off.
  - Edits: `set` (a control to a `value`) and `switch` (a control to its full
    value, open or on, or back to rest with `on: false`).
  - A part turned away from the viewer has no place on screen (a car's
    headlights in profile); the error names the anchors to aim at instead.
  - Works with `surfaceScript`: `{ do: 'grab', target: { surface: 'home', anchor: 'anchor:door' }, then: { surface: 'home', edit: 'switch', anchor: 'control:door' } }`.
- **Home Time demo**: a figure comes home at dusk, opens the door and the
  windows light up.
- **Catalog**: a `prop` surface with its places and edits.

## Changed

- **Docs**: a figure in front of a prop walks on the prop's front line (a
  house's `anchor:doorstep`), not on the prop's own `y`, where it is inside
  the prop's footprint and seems to walk through its walls. Added to
  `docs/acting.md`, the LLM guide and the agent skill.
